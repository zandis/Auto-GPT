## Summary

- 2 Blockers, 7 High, 10 Medium, 4 Low (23 findings).
- Protocol: the arithmetic is right, but the P1 sample is sized for half of its success rule, and P2 measures a rate that stays low even when many eligible patients are lost. Not ready for the IRB.
- Intended use: the logic is sound, but two conditions contradict the plan (the clinician's own-patient view, the reminder card), and "written view" mixes a consultant's letter with the authorities' view. Not ready to rely on.
- SNOMED inquiry: the facts are right. It needs the address, the legal entity and three sharper questions, then it can be sent.
- Overall verdict: fit with changes; none of the three should go out as written.
