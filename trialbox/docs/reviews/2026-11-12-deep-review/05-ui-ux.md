How the console was brought up, because it is itself a finding (UX-005): three starts failed before the fourth worked.
(1) the scratch root was not reachable by the `postgres` user (machine set-up, not the product; the cluster went to `/var/tmp`);
(2) `FileNotFoundError: FHIRHelpers-4.0.1.cql not found in the cql-translator jars`;
(3) `ReportError: font NotoSansJP-Regular.ttf not found`. With `TB_CQL_TRANSLATOR_LIB` and `TB_FONTS_DIR` set by hand the seed took 609 s on the shared machine.

## 1. Reading in ten lines

1. The interface is in much better shape than on 2026-11-10. axe-core found **no violation of any impact on 355 loads**; every page has one `h1`, `html lang` is right, no input lacks a label, no phone page scrolls sideways, **no target under 44 px on 177 phone loads**, text under 14 px is 0.02 % of characters.
2. The three catalogues are complete and consistent: 2,841 keys in each of en, ja, zh-TW, 0 missing, 0 placeholder mismatches, 9 values equal to English (all proper names), no simplified-Chinese character in zh-TW, every zh-TW character is in Big5-HKSCS and every ja character in CP932.
3. Evidence is now shown with value, date and code (`9.43 mg/dL · 2026-06-04 · LOINC 3084-1`); met / not met / unknown / needs a person are four different words and four different colours. This was the main clinical gap last time and it is closed, except for derived values (UX-014).
4. The biggest speed defect is on the desktop, the device a hospital actually uses: on the week list at 1366×768 the Approach / Need info / Not suitable buttons sit about 2,000 px below the top of the patient panel, under all 24 criteria (UX-001). The phone sheet has them sticky; the desktop does not.
5. The outputs that leave the screen are behind the screens. The screening-log workbook is English machine codes in every language (UX-003). PDFs pick one font by report language, so a Japanese word in an English or zh-TW report, or a Chinese quote in a Japanese report, loses common characters such as 検 査 験 薬 or 查 值 啟 (UX-002, reproduced).
6. Legacy encodings are handled well for site sheets, and not at all for three other uploads: a Shift_JIS REDCap dictionary is accepted with HTTP 200 and every Japanese label becomes U+FFFD, with no message (UX-004, reproduced).
7. Error pages are good now: heading, one plain sentence, why, three ways on, the administrator's address, all in the user's language. The remaining hole is the expired session: no message, the typed note is lost, and the user is not taken back (UX-007, open since the last review).
8. The demo is still hard to start outside Docker and its clock has drifted again: on 2026-11-12 the PI's list says "data as of 2026-11-17" and "run of 2026-11-18" (UX-005, UX-006).
9. Novice path: a PI lands on the week list with zero clicks and can mark in two; the first-run wizard is clear and in three languages. But an empty box's sign-in page tells the owner to run a `docker compose` command and never mentions the wizard (UX-011), the authenticator has no QR code (UX-012), and the user guide is English only and not linked from Help (UX-013).
10. Server time is the thing to measure on real hardware: the week list and both home pages took 1.0 to 1.3 s for 79 listed patients here (machine loaded), so every mark is a 1.3 to 1.5 s round trip (UX-010).

Counts: Blocker 0, Critical 0, High 6, Medium 11, Low 6.

## 8. What is good

- Accessibility basics hold under an automatic walk: 0 axe violations, labels, one `h1`, landmarks, visible focus (3 px outline), skip links, 44 px targets, 15 px text, dark-mode colours, print CSS, no third-party asset, a strict CSP.
- Catalogue discipline: 39 files, three languages, equal key sets, equal placeholders; clinical terms follow GCP-J and Taiwan usage; no simplified characters.
- The patient page: why this patient fits in one line, each criterion with the protocol's original one click away, value + date + code, the test-model pill that says what it cannot tell, "reading aid, the investigator decides".
- The phone sheet for marking: Back, Next, three docked buttons, Undo toast; a reason is required for "Not suitable".
- Error pages and upload refusals: translated, specific ("the protocol file is empty, choose the file again"; "PDF, DOCX, TXT or MD, up to 50 MB"), with a way on and a person to ask.
- The first-run wizard: plain questions, pre-filled time zone, specific errors, three languages before any account exists.
- Site-sheet intake already understands Shift_JIS, Big5, 和暦, 民國 and full-width digits and shows what it corrected.
- The SAE register takes the regulator from the site, not from the reader's language.
