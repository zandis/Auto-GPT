## 1. Reading in ten lines

1. The gateway work since the last review is real. The cross-role fallback (DR1 LLM-002) is fixed and holds under 11 fault cases run here: every fault ends as `unknown`, never as agreement. The 16-byte fake weights are refused (DR1 LLM-007).
2. The largest open risk moved from the gateway to **what the models are shown**. The cascade cuts a long note to "evidence sentences plus the note's start" and joins them with no marker. Run here: a Japanese note with `肺結核。` under `【家族歴】` reaches the models as `【既往歴】\n肺結核。`, with `left_out = 0`. Both models read that same text. This is a silent wrong exclusion path (LLM-001).
3. Note reading works only for the demo vocabulary. The built-in lexicon is 32 English words. A criterion outside it, or written in Japanese or Chinese, gets no lexicon hit; SCREEN then ranks sentences with a hash embedder that is hard-coded, and the models read the first five sentences of the note (LLM-002).
4. The accuracy claim "no false model exclusion" rests on one synthetic study, two note criteria, Japanese template notes: 464 of 464, upper 95 % bound 0.65 %. The zh-TW half of G1 never ran. On public data the same pair, with another prompt, finds about half of the true exclusions and wrongly excludes 1.3 to 5.1 % of open patient-trial pairs (LLM-006).
5. No build and no hardened prompt is qualified. `eval/qualification/` holds no record. In PRODUCTION every prompt resolves to the version before the injection paragraph (`judge` v4, not v5), and a champion cannot be promoted. G1 ran on "synthetic weight pins", so the real pin path has never run on real weights (LLM-005).
6. The PHI tripwire refuses ordinary zh-TW and ja protocol text (12 of 20 and 5 of 20 PHI-free sentences here, for example `受試者曾接受過…`), and misses 20 of 61 synthetic identifiers (bare names, katakana names, full-width digits, Japanese insurance numbers, US phones) (LLM-003, LLM-004).
7. There is no calibration. Confidence is the model's own number; the floors 0.5 and 0.75 and the canary thresholds are constants with no measurement behind them (LLM-009).
8. Token counts are an estimate in three copies that disagree; none is the served tokenizer. The shared-quota builds (CGMH's platform) have no `context_length`, so the gateway's size check is off there (LLM-007, LLM-008).
9. Throughput: G1 gives 7.2 s per note-stage patient and 0.78 model calls per second with 16 slots configured. That is 2.4 h per study at 50,000 patients and about 24 h at 500,000. The record has no tokens per second and no latency percentiles, so the cause of the low rate is unknown (LLM-012).
10. Serving flags are copied from one measured run: `--enforce-eager` everywhere, structured output backend `auto`, 40 % of the PRO 6000 card unused. None of their costs is measured (LLM-013).

### 3.1 Detail by ID

**LLM-001, the run.** `exp_excerpt.py` builds one note: a heading `【現病歴】`, 59 daily lines, then
`【既往歴】 高血圧症。 脂質異常症。 【家族歴】 肺結核。 糖尿病。 【アレルギー】 なし。` each on its own line (2,355 characters, 2,222
estimated tokens; the cap for one note is 1,250). The lexicon for a tuberculosis exclusion is `tuberculosis, 結核, 肺結核, tb`,
so the one evidence sentence is `肺結核。`. Output of `_Run.excerpt_set`: `left_out 0`; the shown text contains
`既往歴` and not `家族歴`; its last lines are `…入院33日目、…。\n【既往歴】\n肺結核。`. Cause: `_clip` takes the evidence
sentences, then walks the note from its start and adds every sentence that still fits (`continue`, not `break`), so a
short heading far down the note is added after long lines stop fitting; the result is sorted into note order and
joined with `\n`. `left_out` counts a clipped note only when an evidence sentence was lost (`engine.py:618`). The
second case (zh-TW, `病人否認下列病史：` followed by a list) loses the denial line the same way. What was not run:
the two models' answer on these texts. The text itself leaves little room: a reader who sees "past history:
pulmonary tuberculosis." answers yes. The S2b path has the same clip (`engine.py:748`).

**LLM-002, why G1 passed.** GZQO's two note criteria are about gout flares and a response to treatment; `gout`,
`flare`, `attack`, `urate`, `response` are in the 32-word table with zh-TW and ja equivalents. `cfg.lexicon` (the
"approved site table") is never filled by SCREEN (`grep "lexicon=" services tools`: no caller). The stored-vector
ranking (`vec.s2b`, T-203b) only picks excerpts and is behind a flag. `embed-service` with bge-m3 exists but the
cascade does not call it (`s2b_semantic.py` docstring: "the embedder is injected").

ir_extract: phi_tripwire); send the protocol again")` (`criteria_compiler/service.py:225-227`). Sending again gives
the same refusal. The sentence `受試者曾接受過…` ("subjects who have received…") is standard wording in zh-TW
criteria; `曾` is a surname in the dictionary.

**LLM-005, what "synthetic weight pins" means.** `tools/scenarios.py:170-184` (`weight_pins`, `installed_registry`): the cloud

**LLM-012, the arithmetic.** From `hospital-ja.json`: share of patients reaching the note stage 1,193 / 50,000 =
2.39 % (one synthetic study); calls per note-stage patient 6,704 / 1,193 = 5.6; rate 6,704 / 8,584 = 0.78 calls/s.

With W = 8 h (an assumption; the window is a site setting): 50,000 × 1 study = 0.33 calls/s (fits, 2.4× spare);
50,000 × 5 = 1.66 (2.1× over the measured rate); 500,000 × 1 = 3.3 (4.3× over); 500,000 × 5 = 16.6 (21× over). The
note-stage share on real data is unknown and may be several times 2.39 %.

- **Two note paths** with different retrieval and budgets: v1 `NoteJudge._chunks` (k = 5, context-sized budget) and

- **The `degraded:` column** (unread since DR1).
- **Candidate builds with nothing behind them** in `models/registry.yaml`: `nemotron-3-*`, `qwen3.5-35b-a3b`,
  `gpt-oss-120b`, four `or-*` hosted rows, `ministral-3-14b`. Move bake-off rows to a bake-off file; the registry

- **`tools/bakeoff.py` on OpenRouter** as evidence for box models (DR1 LLM-030): keep `judge_bakeoff` / `qualify`.

## 9. What is good

- The pair rule is one function, and every fault tried here ends in `unknown`. The verifier never stood in for the
  judge in any of 11 fault cases.
- Patient routes have `fallback: none`; a 400 or 404 ends the call; `tb_llm` refuses an answer from an unexpected
  alias. The tests for this are specific and pass.
- `pin_model` refuses weights that are not the expected release, with or without `--trust-first-use`.
- The prompt registry binds a request to a registered text and schema, and PRODUCTION refuses an unqualified
  version. That it now holds back the hardened prompts is the design working; the missing piece is the record.
- "Do not infer from absence" and the suspected / resolved rules in the judge prompt are clear; no few-shot
  examples means no example leakage; sampling is forced by the gateway.
- `left_out` reaches the tier: a flagged "met" is never tier high, a "not met" with notes left out is never final.
- A model's words no longer reach audit records or job errors on a schema violation (codes only).
- vLLM is pinned by digest, behind `model_guard`, with request logging off, on its own network.
- `qualify` has the right steps (schema, longest prompt, determinism, injection, pair gate, throughput).
