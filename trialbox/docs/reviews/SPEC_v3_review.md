## 3. New findings

### Blockers

**N-01 · Three incompatible statements of the S1 missing-data rule.** §5.2 lines 755–757: an inclusion atom with no record under `absence_of_evidence` gives **false**, and becomes unknown only when Atlas coverage < `coverage_threshold`. CAS-11 line 566: "Missing data MUST yield `unknown` and pass to the next stage"; only the index condition may exclude by absence, and only after the S2 rescue sweep. ADR-V3-08 and S1-7 promise v1 rulesets re-run **byte-identically**, but CAS-11 is a runtime cascade rule (R1a), not an IR-version rule, so it changes v1 outputs (v1 fails an inclusion with no record). An engineer cannot write S1-7/S1-8 tests from this. *Replacement text for §5.2 bullet 2:* "For a ruleset compiled under IR ≥ 2.0.0, an inclusion atom with no matching record evaluates to `unknown` at S1 (CAS-11) and is decided at S2 by the note pre-filter: zero approved-lexicon hits in the window ⇒ `fail` (absence_of_evidence) or `unknown` (evidence_of_absence); the index-condition exception applies. `coverage_threshold` governs only `hard` filter-class value comparisons. Rulesets with IR < 2.0.0 keep v1 semantics unchanged (ADR-V3-08) and are recompiled to IR 2 at their next G1." Delete the sentence at 755–757 that gives `false`.

### Major

**N-05 · The recall certificate's statistics are wrong in the example and uninformative by design.**
1. §5.6 example (859–869): strata 15,100/150/0, 2,900/150/1, 240/60/0 with Bonferroni α/3 give a point estimate of **19.3** missed and an upper bound of **537**, not "13" and "≤ 520" (those are the research memo's 12,000/6,000/2,000 example). The per-stratum bounds are 2.69 %, 3.96 %, 6.60 %.
2. With SAFE-2's defaults (150/150/60) the S1 stratum bound is ≈2.7 % × 15,100 ≈ 400 missed for every study, every month, regardless of the truth; against 65 found that is a recall floor of 0.11. Claim (a) will never be informative at n = 150. Appendix F's own table says n = 299 for a 1 % claim, and that table is at α, not α/K, so "configurable from the table in Appendix F" is inconsistent with SAFE-3's Bonferroni.
3. SAFE-3(b) needs r ≥ 59 known positives **per study × ruleset**; the go-live gate (§11.3) needs it on ≥ 5 closed studies. Most Taiwanese site studies enrol < 20; a blinded chart-review alternative at 1 % prevalence means ≈ 5,900 charts per study. Unmeetable as written.
4. A stratum is missing: non-index note criteria excluded at S2 by zero lexicon hits (§4.4 "zero hits → absence semantics"). Lexicon gaps in code-mixed notes are the most likely source of misses and are not audited.
5. The example certificate carries "≤ 520 missed" and "recall ≥ 95 %" side by side with no statement of population; the (b) source may be "historical enrollees (biased)" and the statement still says "at least 95 %".

*Replacement design (SAFE-2/3, §5.6):* Audit per **canonical criterion**, not per study: because `canonical_hash` is shared across studies (CAS-5), excluded patients are pooled across all recruiting studies that use the criterion, samples accumulate month over month under a stored seed (cumulative n, not 150 per month), and the elusion rate per criterion is multiplied by each study's stratum size. Known positives are pooled per therapeutic area and per criterion type (one patient enrolled in any study is a known positive for every criterion they passed); r ≥ 59 becomes a portfolio-level target. Add stratum `S2-zero_hit(note)`. Report (a) as "missed per 1,000 excluded, upper 95 %" per criterion and (b) as "known-positive recall ≥ x on population P" with P named; the statement template takes P. Fix the example numbers or regenerate them from the code. Add to `recall_cert` suite: the bound is computed from the printed strata by the shipped function (no hand-typed numbers).

## 4. Further innovations (only where clearly better)

**I-A · Canonical-criterion recall ledger.** Store elusion evidence per `canonical_hash`, not per study: every audit sample, every five-minute review and every screen-fail outcome is a labelled (patient, criterion) pair that counts for every study sharing the criterion, so recall evidence accrues like the judgement cache and a new study inherits bounds on day one. Builds on CAS-5 and fixes N-05 at no extra CRC cost. Measure: median n per criterion stratum after one quarter; share of new studies with a certified bound at first packet.

**I-B · Importance-weighted elusion sampling.** Sample excluded patients with probability increasing in the S3 nonconformity score and the S2 hit-count margin, and weight by inverse inclusion probability; add a 5 % uniform floor for unbiasedness. At the same n this tightens the bound several-fold where misses actually concentrate (near the decision boundary) and gives the lexicon-gap stratum (N-05.4) a cheap signal. `recall_cert` suite: planted misses near the boundary; coverage ≥ 95 % of seeds.

**I-C · Champion/challenger shadow pass.** Because the V pass is idle after S4 most nights, run a candidate model build on the day's cached (pid, criterion, chunk-set) pairs and accumulate agreement against adjudications, per language stratum. A G5 model change then arrives with weeks of site-specific evidence instead of a one-off eval campaign. Registry entry `role: shadow`; manifests record both SHAs; the QA pack shows the challenger's stratum gaps.

**I-D · Alliance language packs.** The approved site lexicon rows (surface → concept, language, rounded support) and sentence-level de-identified assertion exemplars contain no PHI. Package them as signed "TW clinical shorthand packs" exchanged between alliance boxes by email (v1 COHORT channel), reviewed at G0 like mined rows. The second hospital starts with the first's abbreviations; no US/EU vendor can accrue this. Measure: S2 hit rate and `judge_ml` mixed-stratum sensitivity at a new site before and after import.

**I-E · QBCB stopping point on the `potential` list.** Rank `potential` candidates by nonconformity and tell the CRC "review the top k; beyond that the chance of another candidate is below the certificate's target" (Lewis–Yang–Frieder QBCB, dropped from the research memo's REC-5). Costs nothing and converts the recall target into a weekly workload bound.
