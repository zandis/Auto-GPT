## 0. The verdict in one page

**The owner's concern is right.** TrialBox is built as if the box sees the hospital. At a real site it sees a *feed*: a nightly export, often without the attending physician, without outside care, without the other hospital, with labs as text and with notes that were never coded. The screening engine handles unknowns well (fail-closed, two-model rule, "needs information"). The *workflow around it* does not. It has no place for the people who actually move a patient from "candidate" to "consented": the sub-investigator, the treating physician, the referring doctor, the CRC's phone call, the 治験事務局's paperwork.

**What the code has.** Five roles (admin, coordinator, investigator, viewer, cro) in `services/console/auth.py:28-29`. Four marks: `approach`, `need_info`, `not_suitable`, `not_now` (`sitestore.py:27`). Five contact outcomes (`sitestore.py:40-46`). An eight-state participant lifecycle with visit windows, AEs and deviations (`datacore/lifecycle.py:40-51`). Seven built-in forms (`datacore/builtin/forms.csv`). Feasibility, screening, approval of criteria, screening log, REDCap sync, site set-up with proposed facts, morning mail.

**What the code does not have, and the documents say it has.** The user guide and design doc describe an "Ask treating doctor" mark (`USER_GUIDE.md` §3; `17-clinical-operations.md` BD-19, FR-SCR-07b). It exists only as three i18n strings (`i18n_step4.py:174, 341, 508`). It is not in `MARKS`, so it cannot be chosen, stored or counted. There is no referral object, no sub-investigator, no delegation log, no staff directory (`/site/people` is a patient-demographics disagreement page, `site_pages.py:561-593`), no IRB document tracker, no training record, no drug accountability, no payment, no monitoring visit, no close-out. The owner's 2026-11-09 decisions (referral via the treating physician, sub-PI by delegation, unknown treating physician never stalls a referral) are decided but not built.

**The one design change that matters most.** Treat "the box does not know" as the normal case, not the exception. Every page that shows a fact must be able to show *who to ask* and let the user record the answer in two taps without leaving the page. Today that pattern exists for one thing (the CRC confirms a proposed chart fact). It must exist for the treating physician, the outside medication, the death, the opt-out, the lab unit, the referral outcome and the IRB status.

**Where TrialBox should stop.** It is not an EDC, not an IWRS, not an eTMF, not a pharmacy system and not a CTMS for the sponsor. It should *read* those (or hand off to them with a link, a print or a file) and never become a second store of record for what they hold.

**Priority for the first pilot (P0, §5):** the referral workflow with a staff directory; sub-investigators by delegation; a delegation log with training dates; the three missing contact outcomes; "unknown treating physician" on every candidate row; the excluded-and-why page; an IRB approval and consent-version gate before any approach; a one-page "what the box reads and why" for the IRB.

## 3. Missing data is the normal case

The box is a pseudonymised copy of a feed. The table says where it will not know, and how the product should behave in each case. The common pattern: **show "unknown" honestly, name who can answer, let the user record the answer where they stand, keep the provenance of the answer, never block the workflow on the gap.**

| Gap | Why the box will not have it | How TrialBox should behave |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Treating physician** | SS-MIX2 PV1-7/9 and CSV `encounter.staff_id` are often empty, generic (department code) or stale (the admitting doctor, not the one who follows the patient). CGRD views carry no provider. Rotations change the attending every few months | Owner decision (2026-11-09) is right. On every candidate row: "Treating doctor: Dr X (from visit 2026-09-14)" as a *suggestion* with its source, or "unknown". Buttons: confirm, change (type-ahead over the staff directory), "arranged outside TrialBox", "ask the department contact". The referral proceeds in every case. The route (suggested / typed / outside / department) is recorded in the screening log |
| **Outside-hospital care** | Clinics, other hospitals, home care; JP 紹介状 and TW 轉診單 are scanned PDFs at best; no codes | A criterion that depends on history the box cannot see ("no prior biologic", "no cancer in 5 years") must say "hospital records only; outside care unknown" and route to the CRC with a one-line question for the patient or the referring doctor. The CRC's answer becomes a *confirmed fact* with provenance "patient interview / referral letter / outside record", dated and signed |
| **Deaths outside the hospital** | The hospital learns of a death only if a family member calls or a claim is rejected. The feed may show a living patient for years | Never list a patient as a candidate when the last encounter is older than a configurable horizon without saying "last seen N months ago". Let the CRC record "deceased (reported by family on date)" as a fact that removes the patient from every list, with provenance. Show "vital status unknown" rather than assuming alive |
| **The other hospital in a multi-hospital stay** | Transfer in or out; the other hospital's data never arrives; JP 退院時サマリ and TW 轉院摘要 are text | Mark the episode as "partial: transferred to/from another hospital" when the discharge disposition says so (PV1-36; TW 出院狀態 code). Treat criteria that span the gap as unknown with reason `outside_record`. Offer "attach a referral letter" (site set-up chart upload already does this; make it reachable from the patient page) |
| **Free-text-only facts** | ECOG, smoking, menopause, prior lines of therapy, 疑い, "r/o", family history, allergies: in notes, not codes. SS-MIX2 allergy messages are not read (D-137 "not done") | Already the right shape: the judge quotes, two models must agree, else unknown (D-115). Add: a one-tap "confirm from the note" for the CRC on the quote itself, which writes a confirmed fact with the quote as provenance (D-135 does this for uploaded charts, not for feed notes). Show "the box read 3 notes out of 41 in the window" so the reader knows the coverage |
| **Images without reports** | Imaging done elsewhere, or the report is in a PACS the feed does not export; JP RIS/PACS are separate vendors; TW medical images are on the NHI cloud, not in the HIS export | "Imaging present, no report readable" as a distinct state, never "no finding". Route to a radiology question. If a protocol needs a central read, the box records "image sent for central read on date" as a visit item, nothing more |
| **The paper era** | JP EMR adoption at 400+ beds reached 93.7 % only by 2023 [S20]; records before the EMR are paper or scanned; TW similar for pre-2010 records | Every look-back window that starts before the site's "EMR since" date must say so on the funnel and the patient page ("records before 2012 are paper"). A per-site setting `emr_since` (and per department if the site knows it). "No history found before 2012" is unknown, not "no" |
| **Patients who opted out** | JP opt-out posted on the hospital website for 既存情報研究; TW opt-out after the 2022 Constitutional Court judgment (NHI data) and under the 2025 PDPA amendment; the EMR may hold the flag in a vendor field the feed does not export, or on paper at 医事課 | S0 reads a `consent` table. Add a hand-registered opt-out list the office maintains (name resolved to a pseudonym at the door, as a sheet import does), a visible count "n patients excluded by opt-out" on the funnel, and a daily check that the list was applied. Never show an opted-out patient on any list, including "excluded and why" (the fact of opting out is itself private; show only the count) |
| **Lab units** | JLAC10 and local codes without units; CRP in mg/dL vs mg/L; creatinine mg/dL vs µmol/L; HbA1c JDS vs NGSP before 2012-04 (JP); the lookup unit, not the row unit, is used (R-31) | Unit from the row where present; a unit mismatch gives unknown with reason `unit_mismatch`, and a lab-team task "confirm unit for code X" once per site, not per patient. Show the unit beside every value on the patient page |
| **Medications from other clinics (お薬手帳)** | JP: the paper or app medication notebook, and the 電子処方箋 service, hold outside prescriptions; the hospital feed has only its own orders. TW: see the next row | A criterion on concomitant medication must say "hospital orders only". The CRC checks the お薬手帳 at the visit and records "outside medication reviewed on date: none relevant / list" as a confirmed fact. One tap from the visit form |
| **NHI cloud data (TW 健保雲端)** | The NHI MediCloud shows a physician the patient's medications, labs, imaging, surgeries and more across all providers (12 categories, **verified**), but only at the point of care with the patient's NHI card, the physician's card and the institution's SAM card. The box never sees it, and it must not be queried for screening | On any TW candidate row: "NHI cloud not checked" by default. At the approach visit the treating physician or sub-investigator may look and the CRC records "NHI cloud reviewed on date by Dr X: no exclusion found / exclusion: …" as a confirmed fact. Never store the NHI cloud content itself beyond the fact and the date |

Two cross-cutting rules:

1. **Every fact shows its source and its reach.** "From hospital records up to 2026-11-08", "from a note the CRC confirmed", "told by the patient on 2026-10-03", "unknown: outside care". The existing `basis`/trust model (coded, proposed, confirmed) needs two more sources: `person_reported` (patient, family, referring doctor) and `external_lookup` (NHI cloud, お薬手帳), and a date.
2. **An unknown routes to a person, in the page where it is seen.** "Still needed: ECOG (ask the clinic)", "treating doctor (unknown: choose)", "outside medication (ask at visit)". The person who answers records it there. This is the same two-tap pattern as marks and outcomes and should reuse it.
