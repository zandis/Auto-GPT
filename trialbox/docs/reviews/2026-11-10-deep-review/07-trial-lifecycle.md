## 1. Summary

The product covers the front half of the lifecycle well: feasibility counts, criteria compile with review and versions, on-demand and weekly screening, the clinic-day list, marks, referral record, screening number, a subject state machine, visits, AE and SAE register, deviations, REDCap prefill. The back half is thin: amendments, re-consent, randomisation, monitoring, close-out and archive are partial or absent. Multi-study operation is per study only.

The largest gap is not code. **The PoC must show the two sites' real trial types, and the repository holds neither.** The design documents name early Alzheimer's disease for the Japan site and rheumatology for CGMH. There is no Alzheimer ruleset, no Alzheimer synthetic patient, no protocol or ruleset in Japanese, and no rheumatoid-arthritis interventional trial ruleset. The only trial ruleset is one gout phase 3 trial in English. "Dr Wenn" is not named anywhere in the repository.

Findings: **29**. Blocker 1, Critical 0, High 16, Medium 12, Low 0. Of the 16 High, 3 are already registered or carded (LIFE-018, LIFE-021, LIFE-026).

Top 5:

1. **LIFE-001 (Blocker)** Japan's real trial type (early Alzheimer's disease) has no ruleset, no synthetic cohort and no Japanese protocol.
2. **LIFE-010 (High)** After a protocol amendment nothing is re-screened: a "Not suitable" mark and a screen failure made under the old version hide the patient for ever.
3. **LIFE-019 (High)** A subject is walked to "consented" because a later form implies it; no check that enrolment or a visit is not before consent.
4. **LIFE-022 (High)** The 24-hour SAE clock is counted in calendar days: "overdue" first shows two days after the site learned of the event.
5. **LIFE-027 (High)** Screening outputs are deleted after 365 days whatever the study's state; there is no retention hold, no close-out and no archive.

## 5. What is good (do not change)

- **Unknown is first class along the whole path.** The funnel shows unknowns per step (by a run); "Not suitable" and "Not now" never become "screen fail" (`studies.py:287-301`); a medical reason is never guessed (`home.py:829-832`).
- **One screening number, given at Approach, under an advisory lock** (`sitestore.py:373-386`), and mails carry pseudonyms only (by a run).
- **The subject state machine**: one table of legal steps, undo as a new step, a trail with source (`lifecycle.py:40-52`). Keep it; add to it.
- **Criteria versions as git tags with a diff and reuse of unchanged criteria**, and "open every amber row first" before Approve (`approval.py:201-205`).
- **Only the delegated PI approves; administrators and coordinators never do** (`approval.py:193-199`).
- **Approach is refused while a referral waits** (`home.py:866-871`): the treating-physician rule is in code.
- **The SAE register keeps no patient text and one place per fact** (`saerecord.py:21-23`).
- **The consent explainer is a reading aid with quotes, a second model and a human release**, and says it is not the consent record (`scenarios/consent.py:1-20`).
- **REDCap send never overwrites another person's value and reads back what it wrote** (`edc/service.py:11-14`).
