# BH-1: a bug hunt of the product's own source, batch 1

**Reader:** the people who build TrialBox: the owning lanes (G, H, J, K), the integrator and lane R. Findings for
builders, so in one language.

- **Card:** BH-1 (lane X, the owner's bug hunt of 2026-11-22)
- **Date:** 2026-11-22
- **Base:** master `5cca57cbf9f2932c3763022fe5759134c0a6243e`
- **Findings:** [`2026-11-22-bug-hunt-1/findings.json`](2026-11-22-bug-hunt-1/findings.json) (BH1-01 to BH1-13)
- **Probe tests:** `tests/unit/test_bh1_*.py`, ten files. Each probe asserts the safe behaviour and is marked
  `xfail(strict=True)` naming its finding, so it turns the suite red on the day the finding is fixed, until its mark
  is removed. No product file is changed by this card.

## How this was done

Seven areas were read in parallel (console access, the service APIs' access, archives and paths, feed parsing,
SQL and templates, outbound requests, secrets and the safety chain), each claim then re-checked here by reading the
code and running its probe with `--runxfail` (every probe fails for its stated reason, not a fixture). Everything ran
in-process on synthetic data; no outside host was contacted.

A finding is filed only with an entry point a real user or feed reaches, a failing local probe, no decision making it
by design, and no row for it in the register. **The 2026-11-22 review (R22-01 to R22-67) is not on master**, so it
could not be checked against: a finding here may repeat one of its rows. Deduplication was against the register and
`docs/reviews/`.

**Lanes.** The register's lane list has no F; the two findings in lane F's area (masking, audit) are marked `Int`
here, for the integrator to route. BH1-08's owner (the document parser) was not determined and is also `Int`.

### BH1-01 (High): the lake has no route gate

- **Entry point:** the lake's `POST /promote` and `DELETE /holds/{holder}`, called with the criteria compiler's
  service token.
- **Flaw:** `services/lake/app.py` builds its app with no route gate, so every caller in the lake's `CALLERS` entry
  (`libs/tb_common/service_auth.py`: orchestrator, criteria compiler, adapter) reaches every route. The architecture
  review's trust table (`docs/reviews/2026-11-12-architecture/trust.md`) records that the compiler's token reads lake
  notes; it does not record that it can promote a night or release a hold, so this is worse than recorded.
- **Probes:** `tests/unit/test_bh1_orch_lake_routes.py::test_the_criteria_compilers_token_cannot_promote_a_built_night`,
  `::test_the_criteria_compilers_token_cannot_release_a_jobs_hold` (200 where 403 is expected).
- **Worst realistic outcome:** whoever holds the compiler's token (a compromised compiler container) can serve a built
  night no steward accepted, going round D-760, or release a running job's pinned snapshot.
- **Fix:** a route gate on the lake: the compiler gets query, `/chunks/search` and the reads; `/promote`, `/rebuild`,
  `/holds` and `/site` go to the orchestrator and the adapter only. `/rebuild` and `/site` were read, not probed.

### BH1-02 (High): FHIR Bulk pseudonymisation keeps the narrative and reference display

- **Entry point:** a FHIR Bulk export read by the night's ingest (`adapter` source `fhir_bulk`, also the backfill).
- **Flaw:** `pseudonymise_bulk` in `services/adapter/pipeline.py` drops a Patient's `name`, `telecom`, `address`,
  `photo`, `contact` and `identifier`, but keeps every resource's `text` (the narrative `div`) and a Reference's
  `display` and `identifier`. No decision allows identifiers into the lake.
- **Probe:** `tests/unit/test_bh1_parse_fhir_bulk.py::test_bulk_pseudonymisation_drops_names_and_chart_numbers_from_narrative_and_display`.
- **Worst realistic outcome:** a standard export that fills `Patient.text` or `subject.display` (common) puts
  patients' names and chart numbers into the lake's snapshot, which is meant to hold pseudonyms only, and an
  erasure by pseudonym does not reach them.
- **Fix:** drop `text` from every resource, and `display` and `identifier` inside every Reference.

### BH1-03 (Medium): two reference forms miss the patient's pseudonym

- **Entry point:** as BH1-02.
- **Flaw:** `_REF_RE` in `services/adapter/pipeline.py` matches only `Type/id`. An absolute reference
  (`https://.../Patient/<id>`) is kept with the raw chart number; a versioned one (`Patient/<id>/_history/2`) is
  hashed with its suffix.
- **Probes:** `tests/unit/test_bh1_parse_fhir_bulk.py::test_bulk_reference_forms_reach_the_patients_pseudonym[absolute]`
  and `[versioned]`.
- **Worst realistic outcome:** the record does not link to its patient, so that patient is screened without it (and
  the absolute form also keeps a chart number in the lake).
- **Fix:** take `Type/id` from the end of an absolute reference and strip `/_history/<n>` before hashing.

### BH1-04 (Medium, worse than H34-10): a partial birth date fails the lake build

- **Entry point:** a FHIR Bulk Patient with `birthDate` `1960` or `1960-05`, both valid in FHIR R4.
- **Flaw:** `Flattener.ts` in `services/lake/flatten.py` parses dates with `fromisoformat`. H34-10 records the same
  failure for an invalid date (0000-00-00); this is a valid one.
- **Probes:** `tests/unit/test_bh1_parse_fhir_bulk.py::test_a_partial_birth_date_is_read_not_fatal[1960]` and
  `[1960-05]`.
- **Worst realistic outcome:** one such patient fails the whole night's lake build, so nothing of that night is
  served.
- **Fix:** read a 4- or 7-character date at its precision, or as unknown.

### BH1-05 (Medium): the key-service token follows a redirect

- **Entry point:** sealing and opening with the key service (`kms_seal`, `kms_open`), at the operator's configured
  address.
- **Flaw:** `_kms_call` in `libs/tb_common/kek.py` keeps the default redirect handling, so a 301, 302 or 303 resends
  the token header to any `Location`, over plain http too.
- **Probe:** `tests/unit/test_bh1_egress_kms_redirect.py::test_kms_token_is_not_sent_to_a_redirect_target`.
- **Worst realistic outcome:** whatever answers at the configured address (a misconfigured proxy, a compromised front)
  can move the key-service token to another host in clear text.
- **Fix:** a handler that refuses redirects, as `_NoRedirect` in `tools/trialbox/doctor.py` does.

### BH1-06 (Medium): a two-format chart-number pattern masks neither format mid-line

- **Entry point:** a site's chart-number pattern with an anchor on each branch (`^\d{8}$|^C\d{7}$`), used by the log
  scrubber, the PHI scan and the guard's recogniser.
- **Flaw:** each site calls `strip("^$")`, which removes only the outer anchors, so the inner `$` and `^` stay and the
  pattern never matches inside a line (`libs/tb_common/logging.py`, `libs/tb_common/phi_guard.py` twice,
  `services/guard/recognisers.py`; `tools/support_bundle.py` does the same, not probed).
- **Probes:** `tests/unit/test_bh1_chain_mrnalt.py::test_log_scrubber_masks_both_formats_mid_line`,
  `::test_phi_scan_flags_both_formats_mid_line`, `::test_guard_redacts_both_formats_mid_line`.
- **Worst realistic outcome:** at a site with two chart formats written this way, bare chart numbers reach logs, the
  trace store and model calls unmasked, and the tripwire does not catch them.
- **Fix:** one shared helper that wraps the body as `(?:...)` and refuses an anchor left inside at configuration load.

### BH1-07 (Medium): criterion text becomes live markup in the feasibility mail

- **Entry point:** a feasibility run, started by mail or from the console.
- **Flaw:** the mail's body names the biggest barriers by criterion label or text; `render_body` in
  `services/mail_gateway/outbound.py` turns the body into HTML with a converter that passes inline HTML through. The
  run's PDF escapes the same label.
- **Probe:** `tests/unit/test_bh1_inject_mailhtml.py::test_feas_mail_html_shows_a_criterion_label_as_text`.
- **Worst realistic outcome:** a protocol author or whoever edits a criterion label can put a live link or a remote
  image into a mail sent from the box's trusted sender to study staff and the aggregate recipients.
- **Fix:** escape the body before conversion, or switch off raw HTML in the converter.

### BH1-08 (Medium): a protocol DOCX is inflated with no cap

- **Entry point:** `POST /pilot/protocol` (and `/pilot/consent`, `/pilot/case`) in the console and a mailed
  attachment, both reaching the document parser's `POST /parse`.
- **Flaw:** `_lite_docx` in `services/doc_parser/parser.py` opens the package with no member, total or ratio cap; the
  rule-set zip has all three (`libs/tb_common/zipcap.py`, D-1102). The spreadsheet path is likely the same (not
  probed); the other document backend was not run.
- **Probe:** `tests/unit/test_bh1_paths_docx_inflate.py::test_a_docx_far_over_the_ratio_cap_is_refused_before_it_is_read`
  (a small document far over the ratio cap is read, not refused; the probe stays under 150 MB).
- **Worst realistic outcome:** a signed-in requester or an allowed mail sender uploads a file under the 50 MB cap that
  inflates to many GB, exhausting the parser's memory so that parsing stops for every job. No data leaks.
- **Fix:** run `zipcap.check_infos` on the package before it is opened.

### BH1-09 (Low): audit verification raises on a non-object line

- **Entry point:** `tools/audit_verify.py`, the console's audit verify page and the nightly signer, on an audit file
  with a line that is valid JSON but not an object.
- **Flaw:** `verify` in `libs/tb_common/audit.py` calls `rec.pop` before checking the type.
- **Probe:** `tests/unit/test_bh1_chain_verifyshape.py::test_verify_reports_a_non_object_line` (three cases).
- **Worst realistic outcome:** it fails closed, but with a traceback that does not name the bad line.
- **Fix:** record "not an object" for that line and go on, as for invalid JSON.

### BH1-10 (Low): another spelling of a model host passes the egress check

- **Entry point:** the operator's `EGRESS_EXTRA`.
- **Flaw:** the check that only the gateway reaches a model host in `deploy/egress.py` compares the host exactly, so an
  upper-case spelling or a trailing dot passes and the rule is rendered.
- **Probe:** `tests/unit/test_bh1_egress_model_host_spelling.py::test_another_spelling_of_a_model_host_is_refused`
  (two cases).
- **Worst realistic outcome:** through the operator's own setting, a container other than the gateway reaches a model
  host without the gateway's data-class routing.
- **Fix:** lower-case and strip the trailing dot on both sides, as the EDC host already is.

### BH1-11 (Low): the NHI host skips the model-host check

- **Entry point:** the operator's `NHI_TWPAS_BASE_URL`.
- **Flaw:** `deploy/egress.py` opens an orchestrator rule for that host without the model-host check the EDC host
  gets. The mail hosts are likely the same (not probed).
- **Probe:** `tests/unit/test_bh1_egress_model_host_spelling.py::test_the_nhi_host_is_not_a_model_host`.
- **Fix:** the same model-host check as the EDC host.

### BH1-12 (High): a study-scoped account reads every study's audit list

- **Entry point:** `POST /audit/verify` in the console, by a steward or privacy-office account limited to some
  studies (any role can be given study scope on the account form).
- **Flaw:** the scope check in `services/console/app.py` refuses cross-study lists to a scoped account by exact path
  (`/audit`), while `audit_verify` in `services/console/audit_page.py` renders the same newest-first list of every
  study. D-380 says a study-scoped account sees no audit. `/audit/patients/{pid}`, `/audit/report.csv` and
  `/audit/provenance` are also outside the check (read, not probed); the steward view of break-glass grants and the
  cross-study `/data/*` pages look the same (not probed).
- **Probe:** `tests/unit/test_bh1_console_scoped_audit.py::test_a_scoped_steward_gets_no_cross_study_audit_list_from_verify`
  (200 where 403 is expected).
- **Worst realistic outcome:** such an account, refused at `GET /audit`, reads other studies' audit lines (actors,
  events, study ids).
- **Fix:** refuse `/audit` and everything under it (a prefix, not an exact path) for a scoped account.

### BH1-13 (Medium): the language switch does not use the one way-back rule

- **Entry point:** `POST /lang` in the console.
- **Flaw:** `set_lang` in `services/console/app.py` checks `back` with its own `startswith("/")` instead of
  `services/console/local_path.py`, though D-1156 says the one rule covers the language switch.
- **Probe:** `tests/unit/test_bh1_console_lang_back.py::test_the_language_switch_checks_its_way_back_with_the_one_rule`.
- **Worst realistic outcome:** none today: a backslash way back is redirected but percent-encoded by the framework, so
  no one leaves the site; the decision's claim is untrue.
- **Fix:** `target = local_path(back, "/")`.

## Suspected, not proved

- The same redirect-with-token pattern as BH1-05 in host tools (`tools/trialbox/platform.py`,
  `tools/chat_template_check.py`, `tools/trialbox/noisy_neighbour.py`): addresses from the operator; not probed.
- `support_bundle.mrn_pattern` reads only `deploy/settings.yaml`, so a proof-of-concept box's bundle may get no
  chart-number scrub: not probed.
- A study's EDC link may name a connection another study links (`study_api.py` `put_link`): the console's input
  reached only the other project's title and count; no probe of a leak.
- The adapter's `POST /terminology/load` reads any path in its container; only the console, orchestrator and
  operator call it: not probed.
- `FsStore._path` (`libs/tb_common/objstore.py`) does not contain a key with `//` inside the store; every key traced
  is built by the box from a cleaned name: no reachable entry found.
- A forged backup could restore write bits the extraction filter removed; D-968 already accepts forged backups as a
  threat and only an administrator restores: not probed.
- The lake's SQL guard refuses table functions by a deny list (some catalogue functions pass); the connection is
  read-only with external access off and holds only lake tables: nothing reachable found.
- An impossible death date rejects the patient's whole SS-MIX2 row: by design per STD-025 and D-1100; the birth-date
  case is H34-11.

## Clean areas (read and probed)

- **Console sessions:** no route answers without a session beyond the public ones; every non-API POST form refuses a
  missing CSRF token for all seven roles; login issues a new token; cookies are HttpOnly and SameSite=Strict, Secure in
  PRODUCTION; logout, disabling and a password change delete the session; token, CSRF and sign-in state comparisons
  are constant-time; tokens come from `secrets`; `/studies/{rs}/*` answers 404 outside the account's studies.
- **Service APIs:** the orchestrator's caller resolution, `route_callers.yaml` and `route_actors.yaml` (every route in
  one group), `service_auth.py` (constant-time, fails closed outside DEV and TEST), the actor claims, grants and job
  routes.
- **Archives and paths:** backup and restore extraction (`filter="data"`), the update's signature check, the support
  bundle, the log archive, rule-set import with its caps, upload name cleaning and the console's 50 MB read cap.
- **Parsing:** the XML readers refuse a DTD or entity; the HL7, SS-MIX2 and validator expressions stayed linear on
  50 000-character lines; the CSV source is bounded; no unsafe YAML, `eval` or pickle on feed data.
- **SQL and templates:** the lake's SQL guard against a second statement, comments and string forms; autoescape is
  on in the console; reports escape stored text; mail headers refuse CR and LF.
- **Outbound:** the guarded HTTP transport (address pinning, redirects re-checked, spelling normalised), the EDC
  client, sign-in, the EDC back end. There is no FHIR bulk fetcher; the source reads a local folder.
- **Audit and the safety chain:** the chain, head anchor, signed heads and rotations; the matching verdict and the
  held-back rule; opt-out fails closed; an unreadable hold counts as held.
