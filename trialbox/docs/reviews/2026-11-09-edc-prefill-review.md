| Field | Value |

| Scope | The EDC prefill feature of commit `ba47ced` (D-160): `services/edc/`, `services/orchestrator/edc_api.py` and the `edc_of` wiring in `services/orchestrator/app.py`, `services/console/edc_pages.py` with its templates and texts, the `edc_allowed` part of `libs/tb_common/authz.py`, the `crf_prefill` prompt, schema, role line and stub handler, `tests/edc_kit.py`, `tests/unit/test_edc.py`, `tests/console/test_edc_pages.py`, stage 16 of `tools/e2e_simple.py`. | Method | Read-only and adversarial. Every file in scope was read in full. D-160 was treated as a set of claims. The two test files were run (45 passed). Findings marked "reproduced" were shown with a throw-away probe on synthetic data against the test kit and the REDCap simulator; the probes were not kept. Findings marked "by reading" were not run. ## Summary

- 28 findings: 1 Blocker, 10 High, 14 Medium, 3 Low.
- Verdict: **not ready for validation.** The review rules hold when every call comes through the console in the simple case. They do not hold at the orchestrator, which is where D-160 says they are enforced.
- The Blocker: the orchestrator signs for any user name its caller sends. The signature is only as strong as the service token.
- The High group is mostly "the right value in the wrong place": wrong subject, wrong event, two signed values for one item, an opted-out patient, a citation that no longer supports the value.
- The core rules that were tested (off by default, no confirm without a citation, absence never becomes "No", nothing sent unsigned, a changed value needs a new signature) were checked and hold.

## What was checked and found sound

- The switch is off by default, and draft, decide, sign, send and export each call `require_on` in the service (`services/edc/service.py`:133, 172, 200, 216, 283). Switching off keeps the acknowledgement and every change is kept in `switch_event`.
- A draft without a citation cannot be confirmed (`services/edc/store.py`:292-293); the page shows no confirm button for it.
- For coded items the absence of a record never becomes "No": a presence item is drafted only as its yes code, otherwise it stays unknown with a reason (`services/edc/prefill.py`:213-220).
- Proposed and suspected facts do not reach the coded tables of the lake, refuted and entered-in-error diagnoses are dropped there (`services/lake/flatten.py`:137-140 and the loop that follows), and the prefill reads only the coded tables for values.
- Another unit, a censored result, two different results on the chosen day, a result outside the window and a value that fails the field's check all stay unknown.
- A value changed after signing loses its signature and is not sent or exported until it is signed again; the existing test shows it.
- In a classic project without repeating instruments: the send does not create records, lists a value typed in REDCap as a conflict, and reads every value back.
- On the console the signer is the session's own user; the password is checked again, a failure counts toward the lockout and is audited; the forms carry a CSRF token; viewer and CRO accounts get nothing; an admin can only switch off. The `/api/v1` routes use the same functions as the pages.
- The audit events of the steps that are audited carry no clinical value, and the chain verifies in the test.
- The order of elements in the ODM file matches ODM 1.3.2 for the parts read (root, `Study`, `MetaDataVersion` children, `AdminData`, `ItemData` with `AuditRecord` then `Signature`), and the OID references of exported items resolve inside the file.
- No screen-scraping or RPA: the only ways out are the REDCap API client and the ODM file.
