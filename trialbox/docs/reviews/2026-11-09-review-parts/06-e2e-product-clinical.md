## 1. Summary

The box does what plan v4 §5 describes **on the stub, in one process**. All fourteen e2e stages, the backup and update drills and the walk pass at this head, and the path from a pasted protocol to an enrolment report is real code, not mocks, for everything except the five things that matter most in a hospital: the model pair, REDCap, OCR, the compose stack with its gateway and tokens, and mail and time. Those five are stubbed, simulated or frozen in every automated gate (§2). Plan v4 §5 says the platform is not complete until the card run passes; it has not run (D-139). The build is therefore a complete *design* with a complete *stub run*, not yet a complete platform.

Clinically the core loop (list, patient sheet, marks with a required reason and undo, outcome only after the PI's OK, visit list with "Done today", refusal messages in plain language) is in good shape and fixes most of the 2026-11-08 novice review. Six things would hurt a real site in week one:

1. **The chart number is printed to the PI and the viewer again** on every CASE job page, in the mail-thread table (`CASE PUB-ONC mrn=70000054 lang=ja`), without a reveal step or an audit record. The 2026-11-08 blocker F-01 was fixed for the job line and reopened by the "web request" row (P-01).
2. **A fatal serious adverse event drops off the SAE banner and out of the morning mail** the moment it is saved, because only "ongoing" AEs are shown and *fatal* is not ongoing. Death also changes nothing in the participant's state and starts no clock (P-02, P-03).
3. **Excluded patients are invisible.** No page lists the patients the box excluded, with the reason, for a person to spot-check; a wrongly excluded patient (a coded exclusion, a 疑い diagnosis read as a diagnosis) goes unnoticed (P-04).
4. **The PI's list does not prioritise.** On the public site every one of 88 rows reads "4/7 · 2 unknown · to review"; "Ready to decide" is 0. With note-based criteria and a verifier that is off, a real site will look the same (P-05).
5. **Numbers a PI will quote are either unexplained or inconsistent:** the feasibility headline (1.33 consents a month, 16.0 in 12 months) rests on default visit and consent rates named only in §12; the page's validation sentence says "about 6 % of eligible patients lost" while VALIDATION says 3.1 %; the N-12 pass is a projection from a per-criterion rate on English TREC topics, not a measurement of the cascade on ja or zh-TW notes (P-13, P-14).
6. **The data-core page says "nothing is ever written to REDCap"** directly under the write-back switch (P-07), and the same participant state has two names on two pages (已納入 / 已收案) (P-08).

Pilot blockers in the consolidated list (§3): **14**. Of the 32 findings in §4, 1 is a blocker, 9 high.

## 5. Clinical workflow fit (CRC, PI, admin; ja and zh-TW)

What works, seen on screen: the sign-in page has a language choice and a plain lockout sentence with the administrator's address; 404 and 403 pages have a heading and three buttons in the page language; "Not suitable now" refuses without a reason both in the browser (`select.validity.valid = false`) and on the server; Approach gives a toast with 元に戻す; a contact outcome is withheld until the PI's OK ("Waiting for the investigator's decision" shown, no outcome buttons); the participant page refuses a withdrawal without a reason (「未儲存：請選擇原因。」) and lists only legal next steps; "Done today" on the CRC home opens the visit form filled in; entry refusals are per field and neutral; the readiness page says in plain words what is missing with a button each; the practice site is one tap; every page checked had no raw key, no "None", no horizontal scroll, html lang right; English on ja/zh-TW pages is limited to data (practitioner names, the PI's own note) and the study's labels on the feasibility and approval pages (P-06).

Dead ends and confusing states: the excluded tier has no page (P-04); the PI's list has no order (P-05); the Ask page's wrong answer to a natural question (P-15); the feasibility page's §9 sentence (P-29); the two words for one state (P-08); the "never writes to REDCap" sentence (P-07).

Missing confirmations: P-10 (Disable, Keep/Take, Detach, Approve all).

Error messages that blame the user: none found; the texts say what is missing and what to do. The CSRF failure page was not provoked.

## 6. Data quality and harmonisation realism (D-131, D-132, D-135)

Would a novice really get a real site from messy data to green? For the inputs the stage exercises, yes: the readiness page is the best-guided part of the product, each missing item has a button, the "please check" questions suggest an answer, fixes are listed by kind, and the one rule (proposed never counts) is enforced in the lake and the engines (`site_fact` table, `proposed_fact` reason) and tested in `test_one_rule.py`. The e2e stage proves the mechanics with a sheet the same repository wrote.

What would still break it at a real site:

1. **Scans.** OCR has never run (S4); a Japanese 紹介状 or a printed EMR screen with tables will produce quotes the grounding rule cannot find, so facts are dropped and the chart looks "empty". No warning says why.
2. **Charts without a chart number** (referral letters, pasted text from a phone call) are refused (D-135).
3. **Workbooks with several sheets** (one per PI, or "2023" / "2024") import the first sheet only (P-16).
4. **Labs other than five analytes** go one question per column to "please check" or stay as text; eGFR, ALT, CRP, platelets, LDL, HbA1c in % vs mmol/mol are common in oncology and rheumatology lists (P-16).
5. **Dates**: era/ROC/serial/slashed are handled and ambiguous D/M is asked. Not handled: Japanese 和暦 with 元号 missing ("5.3.15" meaning R5.3.15, common in older exports) is "two-digit year → check", fine; a column mixing ROC and Western years is handled per cell. Two-digit years and unreadable dates go to the list. Reasonable.
6. **Codes from the hospital feed** are not on the mapping page (D-135): a site whose nightly CSV carries local diagnosis codes maps nothing in the UI; `tools/mapping_check.py` is a developer command.
7. **Scale**: every decision republishes the lake; at `main` scale that is minutes per tap (P-17).
8. **Real model extraction** is untested; the stub reads its own synthetic charts by rules (S1).
9. **Encodings**: UTF-8 (with BOM), CP932, Big5 are tried in that order for CSV (`intake.py:54`); EUC-JP and ISO-2022-JP CSVs (older Japanese exports) are not; a Big5 file that happens to decode as CP932 is mis-read silently (CP932 is tried before Big5 and both decode most byte pairs). A preview before save (field research M5) does not exist: the file is read and facts are made at once, then shown.
10. **Names**: the name column is dropped by header heuristics (`_CHART_HEAD`, `name|kana|phone…`, `氏名`); a column headed 患者 or 病患 with names in it would not be recognised as a name column and names would reach the fact table. Not verified on screen; **[code]** `messy.py:347-380`.

Verdict: a motivated CRC with a clean CSV dump, a one-sheet list and pasted EMR text gets to green in an afternoon; a site that starts with scans or several-sheet workbooks does not, and the box does not say why.

## 7. Feasibility and screening validity

**Are the PI's numbers explained and honest?** Mostly, with two gaps. The feasibility page states the population, the snapshot, "no model used", the small-cell rounding, which criteria are not counted and why, "value missing is not a no", and (new) the share of counts resting on coded, confirmed and proposed facts on the readiness page and as notes on the FEAS report. The S3 bridge ("why the list is longer than this count") is good. The gaps: the headline consent projection rests on default rates stated only in §12 (P-13); the validation sentence in §11 disagrees with VALIDATION (P-14).

**Proposed vs confirmed vs coded shares:** shown on the readiness page's feasibility table (columns 依據醫院紀錄 / 已從病歷確認 / 不計入（待確認）) **[seen]** and as two FEAS notes when a site has such facts (`feas.note.basis.*`). They are **not** on the main feasibility page or in the PDF/XLSX unless the notes fire, and not per criterion in the sponsor view. The requirement C3 of the field research (counts by basis in the sponsor format) is half met.

**Tiers:** "likely eligible / to review / excluded" come from the cascade (`strong` → high; `potential:*` → review; any unknown demotes high to review; a coded exclusion or `contact_ok` false → excluded), with `tier_high_confidence: 0.75` from the ruleset. The confidence threshold is never shown or explained to the PI; "First check / Second check" is shown per criterion without saying these are two models (P-05, P-25).

**What the N-12 3.1 % pass proves and does not prove.** It proves: on 1,700 TREC CT 2021/22 patient-trial pairs (English US topics), the deployed judge and verifier as served on the RTX PRO 6000, applied per criterion with "both must block", have a per-criterion false-block rate q ≈ 1.97 % (1.65–2.37); raised to the number of model-read criteria per trial and reduced by the audit factor (10 model errors in 30 sampled drops, read by Claude, not a clinician), the **projected** share of truly eligible pairs lost is 3.1 % (1.8–5.0), under the 5 % gate; and the pair catches 50.8 % of excluded pairs. It does not prove: (a) anything about ja or zh-TW notes (N-11 zh-TW "not recorded"; the public site's ja run used the stub; the one pair run on the public site was `--lang en`); (b) the behaviour of the whole cascade (S1 coded filters, S2 lexicon/ranking with real embeddings, S4 second opinion) on a hospital's notes — N-12 is per-criterion judge answers fed into `hospital_sim project`'s `1-(1-q)^k` model, not a run of SCREEN; (c) that the label noise and the "multi-cohort" cases in TREC resemble a Japanese chart; (d) that the raw 9.4 % loss (before the Claude audit) is not the truer number for a site whose labels are clean; (e) anything about proposed-fact extraction. The 50.8 % exclusion catch means half of the truly ineligible pass to a person: safe, but the PI should know the list is long by design.
