**Verdict.** The service-to-service boundary is now sound in code (per-service tokens, caller table, protected reads, fail-closed `TB_ENV`). What remains is exactly the hospital-facing layer the earlier review called "platform": no second factor, no disk-encryption or key custody on the platform the hospitals will receive, an audit chain any of six containers can rewrite, chart numbers still leaving by password-zip mail, backups that default to the same disk, and a document-parsing path running `pypdf 6.19.0` with 18 published advisories. Two new items appeared with the new features: the orchestrator's screening-log export has no actor and no audit event, and the caller-asserted `X-TB-Actor` lets a token holder both impersonate and bypass the reveal rate limit. Counts: **2 Blocker, 6 High, 10 Medium, 5 Low**.

Not fit to hold identified patient data at a site until S-01, S-02, S-04, S-05, S-06 and S-07 are closed; the rest can follow in the first weeks of a synthetic-data pilot.

### 4.5 IEC 62304 / software-as-medical-device positioning

TrialBox is **not** a medical device in Japan (プログラム医療機器) or Taiwan (醫療器材軟體) as long as: it never computes a diagnosis, grade, risk score or treatment suggestion; the investigator decides eligibility; it never writes to the EMR; no patient-facing clinical content exists. The code honours this today (AE severity entered by a person; no scoring; `case_html.py:23`). Implications: IEC 62304/ISO 14971/IEC 82304 conformance need not be claimed, but (a) the hazard log (TB-DS-11) and the two-model/verbatim-quote controls should be kept as the evidence that eligibility support is safe, (b) the disclaimer must be on every surface (S-21), and (c) any future "visit-window alert" or "AE suggestion" must be framed as a GCP task for staff, not a clinical alert, or the position flips in both countries. A written 該当性 consultation is advisable before pharma sales, not before an academic pilot.
