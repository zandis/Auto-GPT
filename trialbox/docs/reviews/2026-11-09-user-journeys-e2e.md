## 1. Summary

The box runs every role's day-to-day work end to end on the public-data site: 37 journey runs (7 journey scripts × ja and zh-TW on desktop and phone, plus en on desktop, the steward journey also as admin, and the cross-role journey in ja desktop, zh-TW phone and en desktop; the final re-run of the cross journey after each blocker was removed by hand) with about 2,100 logged steps. The pages are fully translated (no raw catalogue key, no `None`/`undefined`, no horizontal scroll on a 390 px phone in any run; the only English on ja/zh-TW pages is the REDCap dictionary's own labels and the raw actor id `console:crc`). The marks, contact outcomes, screening numbers, consent / enrolment / visit / AE / deviation forms, lifecycle steps with undo, the REDCap read-back, exports, the set-up wizard, pasted and Word charts, the proposed-fact review, local-code mapping and the admin's users / jobs / audit / notices all work as pages and through `/api/v1`.

What does not: (1) **a new study cannot be started by anyone the admin creates in the console**: the orchestrator permits job requests by sender *mailbox* from `settings.yaml`, so a coordinator account made on `/ops/users` (even one with a mailbox not on the list, or the seeded `admin`) is refused every FEAS/SCREEN with a raw JSON message, and the one seeded coordinator is cut off after 20 jobs a day (J-01, J-02). The new-study cross journey therefore only ran to the end once a fresh account was given a listed mailbox by hand, the SCREEN line carried `pract=` (no screen sets a study's practitioner scope, J-26) and the coordinator had added the built-in forms (J-29); its en run then hit the day's job limit again (J-02). (2) **Role boundaries leak**: a viewer or CRO account gets the patient-level list and the screening log from `/api/v1` although the same page is 403, and can open the audit trail, every job page (the CASE job prints a chart number, R-03), the registry, the feasibility, set-up, sources, forms and REDCap-connection pages (J-03 to J-06). (3) **Two server errors**: a PDF chart crashes the fact store with a NUL byte (`psycopg.DataError`, shown as "try again later") and the hospital-codes template download is a 500 (J-07, J-08). (4) **No screen for the IT work the brief expects**: first-run set-up, backups, update and rollback, logs, network sheet and secrets status are CLI only, and the console shows no version anywhere (J-09). (5) **The data steward has no role and the admin may not do the steward's work**: every data-management action is `role == "coordinator"`; the admin gets no attach form and a 403 on the dictionary API (J-10). (6) Owner decisions of 2026-11-09 are not in the code: the PI has no reveal (D-143), no excluded-patients page (R-07), no SAE acknowledgement and a recovered SAE drops off the banner (R-09) (J-11 to J-13). (7) Account reset / disable, REDCap detach and fact decisions act at once with no confirmation or undo (J-14).

Coverage: 50.4 % of the 49.9 k statements under services, libs and tools were executed by the box, the journeys, `e2e_simple` and the CLI runs (section 5). What was never entered splits into code that needs infrastructure this box has not (GPU gateway, docker update/rollback, HAPI, mail, OCR, SSO), route handlers no screen leads to, developer tools, and a small set of dead or test-only functions (section 6).

Runs: 37 journey runs (6 journey scripts × ja, zh-TW on desktop and phone, en on desktop once; the cross-role journey in ja desktop, zh-TW phone and en desktop). Steps: 2247 in all: 1891 pass, 308 warn (a gap, a missing control or a dubious behaviour, the journey went on), 48 fail (the step could not be done or the box misbehaved). 186 steps took over 1 s.
