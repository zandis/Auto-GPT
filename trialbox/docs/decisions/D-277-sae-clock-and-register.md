**D-277 The SAE clock counts the 24-hour report in hours, follows the study's named rule set, never falls back quietly, and the register is written on the write path (fix group FG-H: CR-008, CR-009, CR-010, CR-011; card T-602: CR-012, LIFE-022, LIFE-023, LIFE-024, FIT-025, UX-005, UX-028, S-16).**

* **A fatal AE is serious (CR-008).** `datacore.lifecycle.is_serious`: the form's Serious = Yes, or a fatal outcome, always; or a "why serious" reason when Serious is not answered (an import). A fatal AE whose Serious radio says No now has a clock, a register case and the alert `serious_mismatch` ("correct the form"). An explicit No with a "why serious" left over is not serious: the form hides that field once Serious is No and keeps its old value (`datacore.sync.save_form`), so a correction to "not serious" must take effect. The orchestrator's acknowledgement and `/aes` use the same test.
* **No quiet fallback (CR-009).** `lc.clock_rules` replaces the box-wide lookup. The study's named set, else `TB_SAE_RULES` (`jp`, `tw` stay as aliases), else the default of the site's country from `site.tz` (`tb_common.derived.country_of`: `Asia/Tokyo` and `Japan` are Japan). An unknown name, a zone of no known country, a set of another country and an unreadable part of `TB_SAE_REPORT_DAYS` are problems (`RULE_ALERTS`) shown as an alert on the register and listed by `/aes` (`alerts`). An unknown name gives no clock and the alert, never the Taiwan clock.
* **Alerts fail closed (CR-010).** (a) The home and week-pick pages say "could not read the SAEs of …" when the orchestrator does not answer, instead of showing no SAE. (b) A "reported on" day that is unreadable, after today or before day 0 does not count as sent: the clock keeps it overdue (`bad_done`), the register shows `bad_sent`. (c) Overdue follow-ups (the rule's and the requested ones) join the overdue first reports in `h.life_reports`, so the home pages, the week page and N2 list them.
* **Leftovers of PR #60 (CR-011).** A fatal AE moves the participant to withdrawn, reason 5 (Death), on the date of death (new field `ae_death_date`, else the day learned, else the onset). `sae_report` has unique indexes: one initial report and one rule follow-up per party and case (version 3). The UTC fallback was closed by D-270 (`console.timefmt.site_zone` raises `SiteZoneError`).
* **The write path (CR-012).** A page view never writes the register. `h.sae_sync(rs)` (set by `console.sae_pages`) brings a study's cases up to date as `system:sync`, with the form's author and time: after an AE form is saved through the console (page or `/api/v1`), after each entry on the case page, after the investigator's acknowledgement, on `POST /studies/{rs}/sae/sync`, and in the daily notice job (`life_mail`) for answers that came another way (REDCap read-back, the data API). A failure of the hook never fails the save; it is audited (`sync_failed`) and the next write catches up. The pages still read every serious AE live, so an AE not yet in the register is on the worklist and in the alert.
* **The 24-hour report in hours (LIFE-022).** A rule of one day is the "at once" report (直ちに, 立即): due 24 hours after the time the site learned of the event (new field `ae_aware_time`, HH:MM in the site's zone). Without a time it counts from 00:00 of the day learned, the strictest reading, and the page asks for the time (`aware_time_missing`). The clock carries `hours` and `due_at`; the pages pass the site-local `now`. Longer rules stay calendar days.
* **Rules per study from named sets (LIFE-023, FIT-U06 part).** `lc.RULE_SETS`: `jp_gcp` (治験, company sponsor: sponsor and head of the institution at once, no regulator clock at the site), `jp_gcp_ii` (医師主導治験: also PMDA 7/15), `jp_crb` (特定臨床研究: CRB and MHLW 7/15), `jp_guideline` (指針 research: head at once), `tw_trial` (人體試驗: sponsor at once, IRB and TFDA 7/15), `tw_research` (人體研究: IRB 7/15). They are plain defaults; the study confirms them with its IRB SOP. The study set-up page has one field; the coordinator who saves it is recorded as the person who confirmed it (`datacore` table `sae_rule`, version 4; audited `life.sae_rule`). Without a choice the country's strictest set applies and the register says it is not confirmed.
* **Regulator from the country (UX-005).** The set names its regulator (PMDA, MHLW, TFDA), shown the same in every language ("Regulator (TFDA)"). The deadline lines carry a legend for "a/b" and "–".
* **Expectedness and unblinding (LIFE-024, part).** New AE form fields (additive): `ae_expected` (per the reference safety information), `ae_sponsor_related`, `ae_unblinded`, `ae_unblind_date`, `ae_unblind_reason`. The regulator clock is off for an expected event (`expected_noreg` on the case).
* **Banner names the rule; office copy; acknowledge on the case page (FIT-025, UX-028).** The worklist and the case page name each study's set, its regulator and who confirmed it. A new case is mailed to `TB_SAE_OFFICE_MAILBOX` (an internal address, through the N2 notice path) with the study, screening number, event, dates, set and deadlines, never the AE term. The investigator acknowledges on the case page. The trail is in words in the reader's language. The case page shows the AE term read from the AE form; the register, the trail, the audit and the mail never keep it.
* **No quiet SAE (S-16).** Every party at 0 leaves a serious AE with no clock: the problem `noclock` is an alert on the register and in `/aes`.
