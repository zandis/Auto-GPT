# D-1160 A second identical action changes nothing, writes no event and sends no mail

- **Task:** the register's open Highs for the console, card TWICE (posted on #639): V11-01 (a second identical Approach), V11-03 (two "send now" at once), V11-08 (a double click on break-glass open), V11-14 (a second SAE acknowledgement), and V11-11's break-glass part (ending an ended grant), sent by the integrator on 2026-11-19. Stacked on D-1159 (the same break-glass store). · **Date:** 2026-11-19
- **Decision:** a second open of the same patient or job, with the same reason and note, while a grant on it is open is answered by that grant (a new justification is a new open, audited) (one person's opens are taken one at a time, `pg_advisory_xact_lock`): no grant, event or mail. Ending a grant ends it once (`UPDATE ... WHERE ended_at IS NULL`); a second end writes nothing. The same person's Approach again on the same list, when the earlier one was already their override under the same warnings, writes no IRB-override record or event (an earlier Approach that was none writes one). The data core's `ack` says whether the tap was new; the orchestrator writes `life.sae_ack`, and both console pages write `console.sae_ack`, only for a new one. Each digest claims its day's log row before it is sent (`claim_notice`: a row with no subject, not shown as received), finishes it after the send, and gives it back if the send fails, so two senders at once send it once; a claim 15 minutes old whose sender died is taken over by the next run. The adversarial review's HIGH (an override skipped after a non-override) and its MEDs (a new justification, a digest lost with its sender) are fixed here.
- **Left:** V11-11's EDC switch part (a Medium) stays open; two Approaches at the very same instant may still both write an override (the earlier mark is read before the store's transaction, the review's other MED); the mark rows stay append-only by design (D-104).
