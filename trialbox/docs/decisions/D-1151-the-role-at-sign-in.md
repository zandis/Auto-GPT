# D-1151 The role is chosen at sign-in and changed without signing in again (STEPS-ROLE-2, amends D-877)

- **Task:** the owner's ruling of 2026-11-17 (18:55 Taipei), through the integrator, amending D-1063 and D-877: "change roles should be drop down in the login page, and once login, in the page right upper need to allow change, no login required again." · **Date:** 2026-11-17
- **Decision:** while acting is on (a test box with `steps.acting_role`), the sign-in page has a role drop-down: the roles the box gave its accounts (it cannot know who signs in, so it never looks a name up before its password), and "the role I used last". A chosen role the account holds becomes its starting role (audited `role.acting`, at sign-in); any other value, a forged one included, is ignored and the person signs in in the last role, else the account's own. Every session starts in the role last acted in (`users.last_acting_role`, kept by each switch). The top bar's switch keeps the session (no new sign-in, no second factor) and lands on the role's home page in the session's language; a page the acting role may not open offers the person's other roles, and that switch returns to the page. D-877's gates stand: extra roles exist only on a DEV or TEST box, so in the product no account holds more than one role and no drop-down shows.
- **Left:** the refused page says the current role may not open it and offers the others; it does not name the one role the page needs (a refusal does not carry the matrix row it asked).
- **Held by:** `tests/console/test_one_sign_in_acting_role.py` (the sign-in drop-down only while acting is on, the chosen role taken, a forged one ignored, the session kept on a switch, the audit row, the refused page's switch), synthetic accounts only (failing output on the previous code kept: `steps2-fail.txt`); the auth migration pin moved on purpose.
