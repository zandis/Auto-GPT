- **Task:** ERASE-A3, lane C2's backlog, on the owner's ruling D-1032 ("study.rc_record, study.rc_write and edc.draft follow the same rule on your later card"). After ERASE-A2 (D-875), REDCap's read-back still joined a kept screening number to a never-consented patient's pseudonym. · **Date:** 2026-11-17
- **Decision:** the `screening_log` clear gives a never-consented patient's `study.rc_record` rows, and every read's stored result in `study.rc_read_log` that names them, the same token as the number's other rows (record id, status, dates and subject number stay). The read-back stores a row of an erased number under the token and never writes its status to the registry; consent REDCap shows for such a number is flagged to the coordinator and the audit trail (`RC-ERASED-CONSENT`), without the token. A number not read back while the link is on, or while a failure switched it off, keeps the rows (unknown). `study.rc_write` holds no pseudonym (number, record, hash of the date and status fields). The commits run lifecycle, site, read-back, registry.
