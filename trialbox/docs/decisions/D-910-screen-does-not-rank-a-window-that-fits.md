# D-910 SCREEN does not rank a window that fits the excerpt budget

- **Task:** note-processing review of lane G's SCREEN run (2026-11-15, 500 patients: about 1,275 s, about 16 min of it S2b embedding on the CPU); **Date:** 2026-11-15; **Status:** reserved
- **Decision:** with absence semantics off (SCREEN), a zero-hit pair goes to S3 whatever its S2b score, and the score only orders the notes the excerpt builder reads first. When every note of the pair's window fits the excerpt budget whole (`matching.excerpts.fits_whole`), the builder shows them all, newest first, whatever that order: the pair is routed to S3 with the same excerpts and no sentence is embedded for it (`CascadeConfig.s2b_skip_when_fits`, on). The S2b stage log counts such pairs (`not_embedded`) and says why (`not_embedded_why`); `pairs_s2b_above` still counts them, as before, since they go to S3. The in-run judge cache key of such a pair is the criterion's text and note question and the window's sentences, so two pairs of a patient share an answer only when their prompts are the same.
- **Not here:** index sweeps (their score decides a rescue), feasibility (absence semantics on: the score decides absence), a stored-vector source (`vec.s2b`, which keeps its own per-patient account), a window that does not fit (the ranking picks what is shown). Nothing the recall certificate reads changes: it reads no S2b field.
- **Test:** `tests/unit/test_s2b_skip_when_the_window_fits.py`; `tests/unit/test_cascade_pipeline.py::test_s2b_is_not_run_where_the_window_fits_and_nothing_changes` (prompts, outcomes, tiers and stage counts equal with and without, on the synthetic site).
