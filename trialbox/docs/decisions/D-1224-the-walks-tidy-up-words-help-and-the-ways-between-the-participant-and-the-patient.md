# D-1224 The life-cycle walk's tidy-up: one word per action, Help per role, the ways between the participant and the patient sheet, one clock for the box (J-4)

- **Task:** lane J, card J-4: the findings of lane H's life-cycle walk (`docs/handover/life-cycle-walk-findings-2026-11-17.md`, D-1135) that are wording, missing links between the participant and the patient sheet, pages absent from Help and the coordinator's guide, the box's clock and the opt-out worker's health line: F-01, F-02, F-03, F-07 (with the link back, half of F-18), F-08, F-09, F-10, F-12, F-19, F-21. · **Date:** 2026-11-17
- **Decision:**
  - **Help per role (F-01).** A page's Help text is `help.page.<slug>.<role>` where the catalogue has one, else `help.page.<slug>` (`console.step4.help_key`); the role guides take the same key, so Help and the guide stay one source (D-774). The weekly page has a coordinator's text: the investigator decides there, the coordinator gets a task and records the contact outcome.
  - **One word for the mark (F-02).** The confirmation after Approach says "Marked Approach" (「声をかける」を付けました, 已標記「邀請」), not "Approved", and the coordinator's hint on the weekly page (`wb.crc_hint`) says a task comes when the investigator marks Approach, not "approves contact"; the button and Help already say Approach.
  - **Which patient (F-03).** The walk plan and the findings page state the rule the walk follows (the first row of the list the weekly page opens on, in the page's order; the later steps follow that patient by its pseudonym). The testers' pages are not changed: they hold H-10's new study only (steps 1 to 11), and the findings page keeps the life-cycle steps off them until the findings that slow a tester are settled. This is the smaller true fix.
  - **The ways between the participant and the patient (F-07, half of F-18).** The screening number is a link to the participant's page where the coordinator's outcome box shows it (`_mark.html` `outcome_box`: the patient sheet and the weekly page's panel), for a role that reads patient data; on a phone the link is a 44 px target (D-133), the desktop look unchanged. It is not yet a link on the investigator's candidate pages (`team_candidate.html`, `team_candidates.html`): those are left to card J-5 (card J-2 is changing them). The participant's page links back to the patient sheet of the pseudonym the number was given to, only for a reader the sheet admits by its own rights (`h.reads`: the `phi.view` row, then the delegation log's "screen"; a sub-investigator ticked only for data entry reads the participant but not the sheet, and sees neither the link nor any part of the pseudonym), and never for an erased pseudonym (D-875: a token is never shown; ERASE-A2's `erased:<day>:<hex>`). A reader who opens the sheet only under break-glass gets no link, on purpose: a link must not invite break-glass. The `/api/v1` twins of the participants' pages hide the same (`pid` blank when erased or when the reader fails `h.reads`); `who` stays as the API has it, for a machine reader. Links only: the merged lists and the search are card J-5.
  - **Who recorded a step.** A step TrialBox recorded itself (a service principal, such as the morning notice's `service:console-notify`) reads "TrialBox" in the participant's trail; a person's step keeps the person's user name.
  - **Participants and the study's state in Help and the guides (F-08).** Both get their own Help text (`help.page.subjects`, `help.page.state`) and a place in the coordinator's and the investigator's guides (as the role-route table opens them). The "More" menu is not changed (cards J-7/J-8); the command field is not changed either, as finding them there takes more than one table line (an intent, its words, its roles and its address).
  - **A participant's page names the participant (F-09):** its heading is `life.subject.title` ("Participant {r}").
  - **One day for the box (F-10).** Both of the orchestrator's clocks take the box's own clock when the box has one (`Orchestrator.clock`): `Orchestrator._now` (jobs' received and updated times, mail records, judged and answer stamps, the daily limits' day, notice days, scenario stamps) and `orchestrator.app._now` (the lifecycle's derived steps, the registry's updates, the study, data and site-setup services, identifiers). Every tool that builds its console with `tools.list_demo.console` (list_demo, cgmh_demo, scenarios, public_e2e) gives it the console's clock, and `tools.demo --keep` does on a reopen. On `--day data` the steps TrialBox records itself, its jobs and the steps a person records then carry the same day. **The product is unchanged:** a box without a clock (every installed box, and the test stack's `TB_TODAY`) keeps the wall clock in the site's zone, and a test holds it.
  - **Side effects, demo and test boxes only.** On a frozen clock (`--day data` while the clock is not live) time does not pass for the orchestrator: its rolling windows never slide on a long-running or `--keep` demo: the hourly caps (`identifiers.admit`, `db.take_limit`) count everything since the box started as one hour, the daily job limit per sender (`Orchestrator.create`, the jobs of the last day) counts every job of the session as one day, and the notice day (`orchestrator.app`, a notice per day) stays the box's day, so a long session can meet a limit sooner than real time would; the stamps on a box clock are strictly increasing (`Orchestrator.box_now`: at least one microsecond after the last, under a lock), so two jobs never share an instant (a MICROBATCH after a SCREEN, a chained ingest) and the day stays the box's; the clock must return an aware datetime (a naive one is refused); the REDCap read-back's `since` follows the box's clock; and a box kept (`--keep`) from a seed made before this change holds stamps of both days until it is seeded again.
  - **Two English words (F-12):** taking back a mark is "Take back my mark" ("Mark taken back"); the participant's "Withdraw" stays. zh-TW and ja already used two words.
  - **The opt-out register's note (F-19)** is labelled "Note for the register" (`team.optout.note`), not the mark's "Note for the coordinator".
  - **The opt-out worker on the Health tab (F-21).** The administrator's Health tab shows a line for the check `/metrics` exposes (`tb_health_check_level{check="optout_worker"}`, `tb_common.metrics.check_optout`): green with the minutes since the last run, amber before the first run, red when the worker is off, its last run failed or no run completed for three intervals, with "What to do". When the orchestrator gives no worker status (down or not reporting) the line says "not reporting" (amber) instead of going missing. The System tile's count takes the line: green only when every service and the worker are, amber when only the worker has not run yet or is not reporting, red when a service is down or the worker is red.
