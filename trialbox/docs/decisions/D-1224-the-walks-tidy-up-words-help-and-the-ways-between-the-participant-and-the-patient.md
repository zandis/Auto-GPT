# D-1224 The life-cycle walk's tidy-up: one word per action, Help per role, the ways between the participant and the patient sheet, one clock for the box (J-4)

- **Task:** lane J, card J-4: the findings of lane H's life-cycle walk (`docs/handover/life-cycle-walk-findings-2026-11-17.md`, D-1135) that are wording, missing links between the participant and the patient sheet, pages absent from Help and the coordinator's guide, the box's clock and the opt-out worker's health line: F-01, F-02, F-03, F-07 (with the link back, half of F-18), F-08, F-09, F-10, F-12, F-19, F-21. · **Date:** 2026-11-17
- **Decision:**
  - **Help per role (F-01).** A page's Help text is `help.page.<slug>.<role>` where the catalogue has one, else `help.page.<slug>` (`console.step4.help_key`); the role guides take the same key, so Help and the guide stay one source (D-774). The weekly page has a coordinator's text: the investigator decides there, the coordinator gets a task and records the contact outcome.
  - **One word for the mark (F-02).** The confirmation after Approach says "Marked Approach" (「声をかける」を付けました, 已標記「邀請」), not "Approved"; the button and Help already say Approach.
  - **Which patient (F-03).** The walk plan and the findings page state the rule the walk follows (the first row of the list the weekly page opens on, in the page's order; the later steps follow that patient by its pseudonym). The testers' pages are not changed: they hold H-10's new study only (steps 1 to 11), and the findings page keeps the life-cycle steps off them until the findings that slow a tester are settled. This is the smaller true fix.
  - **The ways between the participant and the patient (F-07, half of F-18).** On the patient sheet and the weekly page's panel the screening number is a link to the participant's page (for a role that reads patient data); the participant's page links back to the patient sheet of the pseudonym the number was given to. Links only: the merged lists and the search are card J-5.
  - **Participants and the study's state in Help and the guides (F-08).** Both get their own Help text (`help.page.subjects`, `help.page.state`) and a place in the coordinator's and the investigator's guides (as the role-route table opens them). The "More" menu is not changed (cards J-7/J-8); the command field is not changed either, as finding them there takes more than one table line (an intent, its words, its roles and its address).
  - **A participant's page names the participant (F-09):** its heading is `life.subject.title` ("Participant {r}").
  - **One day for the box (F-10).** The orchestrator's stamps (`orchestrator.app._now`: the lifecycle's derived steps, the registry's updates, the study and data services) take the box's own clock when the box has one (`Orchestrator.clock`); `tools.list_demo.run` and `tools.demo --keep` give it the console's clock. On `--day data` the steps TrialBox records itself and the steps a person records then carry the same day. A box without a clock (the product, the test stack's `TB_TODAY`) is unchanged: the wall clock in the site's zone.
  - **Two English words (F-12):** taking back a mark is "Take back my mark" ("Mark taken back"); the participant's "Withdraw" stays. zh-TW and ja already used two words.
  - **The opt-out register's note (F-19)** is labelled "Note for the register" (`team.optout.note`), not the mark's "Note for the coordinator".
  - **The opt-out worker on the Health tab (F-21).** The administrator's Health tab shows a line for the check `/metrics` exposes (`tb_health_check_level{check="optout_worker"}`, `tb_common.metrics.check_optout`): green with the minutes since the last run, amber before the first run, red when the worker is off, its last run failed or no run completed for three intervals, with "What to do".
- **Not here:** F-04, F-11, F-14, F-15, F-16, F-17 (lane C2), F-20 (lane H, the integrator), F-05 and F-13 (card J-2), F-06 (after card J-3), F-18's search (card J-5). The walk plan has no field for a settled finding, so its findings list is left as the walk found it; its table is regenerated, never edited by hand.
