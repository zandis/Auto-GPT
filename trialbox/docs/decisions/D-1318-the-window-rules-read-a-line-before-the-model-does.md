# D-1318 The window rules read a criterion's time expressions before the model's windows are trusted

- **Task:** OPT-1 W3. A graded sample of 297 window lines (public registry text) put the model's windows at 70.4 % right; deterministic rules with the model as fallback read 90.9 % right on a blind second judgement (rules alone 87.2 %, 259 of the 269 lines they claim). **Date:** 2026-11-27. **Status:** reserved (the number is a placeholder; the integrator may renumber).
- **Decision** (`ir_extract/window_rules.py`, the first step of the per-criterion loop in `postprocess`, standard library only).
  - The rules read every temporal expression of the line: value and unit as written, anchor, and kind (`window`, `duration`, `none`, `unparsed`). A line with any expression they do not fully read is `unparsed`: nothing is changed. A line in Chinese, Japanese or Korean is left to the model: the rules read English.
  - **A window the line states is the rule's** when it has a form in days before screening (a side of the anchor, a whole-day unit, the anchor `screening`). It replaces the window of a criterion that has one atom and a different window, and is added when the model gave none. A "no more than N" is a within; a state held "for at least N before" the anchor (a drug-free period) is the N before it, compared but never written: when the atom's window differs the criterion goes to a person. A bare "within N" whose clause speaks of a plan ("plan to move within 1 month") lies after screening; one counted from another event ("synchronous or metachronous (within 5 years)") and an anchor that is a clause ("having received a ...") or holds a second anchor are `unparsed`. Equal means within 2 % (and a day): 12 months is the model's 365 days as well as 360. With several atoms the line does not say which carries the window: the atoms are compared, never written (`window_disagrees`, `window_unattached`). An age or sex atom, and an atom with a `duration` (a course of N days inside its window), are not the rules' to change.
  - **A window the compiler cannot count from the screening date goes to a person** (`window_anchor_unsupported`), whatever window the model wrote: one counted from the study's own dosing or end (`first_dose`, `last_dose`, `end_of_study`, with or without a number: "prior to the first dose"), from another named event (`randomization`, `enrollment`, `consent`, "before surgery"), or after screening. A mixed criterion goes to a person too: its note question does not carry the held window (review 1627.1).
  - Every change or disagreement flags the criterion (`window_rules_override`, `window_disagrees`, `window_unattached`, `window_anchor_unsupported`) and so `needs_review`.
  - **A window the line never states** (no time expression, or only a duration: "stable dose for 3 months" is how long a state has lasted, not a window, STATUS ruling 2026-11-27) is left alone by default. The stand-in extractions the committed rulesets were built from put a recency window on every "latest" lab and on pregnancy (-365, -180, -90, -280 days), which the prompt does not ask for; dropping them (`window_not_in_text`) and refusing a window on a line with no word about time (`window_invented`, check (iii)) are in the code behind `window_rules.DROP_UNSTATED_WINDOWS` (false) and change those rulesets' regenerated output, so they wait for a ruling.
- **IR 3.x:** `Anchor` gains `enrollment`, `consent`, `first_dose`, `last_dose`, `end_of_study` (additive; the two schema files are regenerated). `downgrade_to_v1` refuses them by name (`NotV1Expressible`); `semantics.one` and `semantics.window` refuse them by name (`CompileError`), so a person reads the atom. No compile path for them yet.
- **Compile checks** (OPT-1 §3), in `semantics.window` and `semantics.lower`: (iv) a window of more than 3,650 days, or of more than 72 hours counted in hours, is refused; (v) one length stated as both the window and the atom's `duration.min_days` is refused. (v) is narrower than "duration and window are exclusive": a medication course of N days inside a longer window is the existing, valid meaning of `duration` (`semantics` module doc). (iii) is the lint above.
- **Not chosen:** letting the rules attach a window to one of several atoms; reading a model window as the index-date form of one before randomization, consent or an event; converting hours or minutes to whole days (no whole-day form); reading `enrollment` or `consent` as compile-ready in IR 3 (no ruling).
- **Test:** `tests/unit/test_window_rules.py` (50 hand-written readings and 12 abstentions of public registry text, other readings, the 83 adjudicated pairs the rules read right and the safe outcome of the 4 they read wrong, the reconcile and lint steps, one case per finding of review 1627.1), `tests/unit/test_window_rules_postprocess.py` (the step in `postprocess`, the enum, checks iv and v).
