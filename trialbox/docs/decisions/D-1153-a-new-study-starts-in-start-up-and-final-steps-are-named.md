# D-1153 A new study starts in start-up, and a final step is named before it is made (WALK-1)

- **Task:** lane H's life-cycle walk H-20 (PR #994, `docs/handover/life-cycle-walk-findings-2026-11-17.md`), lane C2's defects, as the integrator ordered them: F-14 (a new study counts as open, so D-415's SAE-type check never ran), F-11 and F-15 (a withdrawal and a close are final after one click; Reason not marked required), F-16 (kept until counted from the UTC day). · **Date:** 2026-11-17
- **Decision:** D-386 point 3 is made true by the console: a protocol sent through it (any FEAS with a protocol attached, the protocol page included) for a study the box does not know records the study in start-up (`SiteStore.begin_study`, audited `console.study_state`), so it opens only through the move to open and its SAE check (D-415). A study the box knows (the pilot studies, a new protocol version) and one with a state keep theirs; "no state counts as open" stays for studies from before D-386, since reading every study without a state as start-up would hold every pilot study (a new study's type is `trial` by default). Withdraw and Screen fail (the final steps that take a reason) and a close ask once on the confirmation sheet (D-143), saying what becomes final (the close with the day its files are kept until); Yes posts the same form with `confirm=yes`; a step without a reason is refused before any sheet, as before. Both Reason fields say they are required. `retention.kept_until` takes the site's zone, and `held_after_close` reads both days in its `now`'s zone (the orchestrator's is the site's), so a close in the site's early morning counts from the site's day.
- **The adversarial review:** the start-up is written once the job is accepted (a refused request leaves no study); RETENTION reads a study whose one row is that start-up as before (D-765: no running study), so a feasibility the site declines ages out; every check of a step (who, a closed study, the record, the day, the criterion, a known reason) runs before its sheet, so no sheet is shown for a step that would be refused.
- **Left:** lane H's walks (H-10's `make walk-new-study`, H-20's `make walk-life-cycle`) run SCREEN on a new study and withdraw and close in one click: they need the SAE type and Open before SCREEN, and Yes after Withdraw and Close (the integrator passes them to lane H); a study created by mail (not through the console) is not put in start-up (the orchestrator does not write the console's table), nor one whose start-up could not be written after its job was accepted (logged; the coordinator records it on the state page); RETENTION cannot tell a declined feasibility from an accepted study still in start-up, so the FEAS files of both age out, as before D-1153.
