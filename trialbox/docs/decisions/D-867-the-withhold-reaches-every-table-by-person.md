# D-867 The withhold reaches every OMOP table by person (ERASE-B)

- **Task:** ERASE-B, lane C2's backlog after the PoC freeze: 12 owed OMOP entries that wait for nothing. · **Date:** 2026-11-16
- **Decision:** the opt-out's withhold (`omop.load.withhold_persons`, D-441) now also deletes, by person and in the same transaction:
  - the 12 CDM tables no loader writes today: `condition_era`, `drug_era`, `dose_era`, `device_exposure`, `specimen`, `visit_detail`, `payer_plan_period`, `episode` with its `episode_event`, `cohort` (by `subject_id`), `cost` (through the person's events, by domain), and `location`, an address no other person and no care site uses;

  It runs on every tick for everyone opted out, before the early return taken when no live records are left, so a loader that writes one of these tables later is covered from its first row. The worker reads each back, except `cost` and `location`, which are reached only through the person and say so in the registry. The export as of a time (T-728) ends in the same function and drops them too.
- **Why:** each was owed with a plain delete by person and needs nothing from another lane. The owed list goes from 49 to 37 on master (this card alone).
- **The adversarial review (NOT SOUND on the first build):** the first build deleted `tb_conflict` too. The withhold is undone by a lift, and a steward's ruling on a disputed item has no before-image. After a lift, the value the steward rejected would stand alone, and the as-of export would wind back wrong. `tb_conflict` stays owed, to be erased at retention or with a before-image. Its other findings were dormant (no loader writes these tables): a `visit_detail`, `Episode` or `Note` cost and a cost linked only through a payer plan period are not reached; `cohort` is taken to hold persons.
- **Left:** `omop.tb_record` and `omop.tb_record_event` wait for the owner's ruling (the integrator advises a D-441 keep entry: ids only, the trail of what was erased). A `cost` row whose event was already deleted on an earlier tick cannot be attributed; no loader writes `cost` today, so there is none.
