# D-911 The embed service reads a long text whole, by windows, and loads at start everywhere

- **Task:** note-processing review of lane G's SCREEN run (2026-11-15: the embed service died at its 4 GB limit); builds on D-900; **Date:** 2026-11-15; **Status:** reserved
- **Decision:** (1) a text over `TB_EMBED_INPUT_CAP` (512) tokens is no longer cut at the cap: it is embedded as consecutive windows of at most the cap (special tokens counted), cut at token boundaries, and its vector is the L2-normalised mean of the windows' vectors. The mean, not the best window: the service never sees the query a window would be scored against, so "best" cannot be chosen there, and the one-vector-per-text answer, the vector caches keyed by text and the stored-vector paths all stay as they are. The mean reads every part of the text where the cut kept only its first 512 tokens. A text within the cap is embedded as it is: its vector does not change. (2) `TB_EMBED_BATCH_BUDGET` falls from 8192 to 4096 padded tokens per encode call: a batch at the cap then holds about 0.35 GB of activations (16 heads x L^2 x 4 B, twice, and the feed-forward layer) beside about 2.9 GB of weights and runtime, inside the 4 GB limit the run died at; on the CPU a bigger batch is not faster. The compose default stays 6 GB (D-890). (3) The client sends at most 64 texts and 100,000 characters per request (a longer text alone), so a request's time follows its tokens. (4) The model loads when the service starts, in every environment (the lifespan, under D-900's one lock), not only in PRODUCTION; requests still encode `TB_EMBED_CONCURRENCY` (1) at a time.
- **Not here:** a GPU or lower-precision embedder (a re-pin and a threshold refit, D-429); torch threads per container.
- **Test:** `tests/unit/test_embed_long_inputs.py` (a 50,000-character sentence, concurrent requests, one load, batches within the budget, the defaults inside 4 GB).
