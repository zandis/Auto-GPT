# D-756 One definition per shape for the note masker and the guard

- **Task:** NEW-B-50 (the first-look session's deep review of real notes, its findings kept out of the repository; placed with lane B2 by the integrator), lane B2 · **PR:** `agent/NEW-B-50-one-shape-each-masker-and-guard` · **Date:** 2026-11-15 · **Status:** accepted

1. `tb_common.phi_guard` defines each shape once, and both the note masker (`note_spans`, `mask_note`: the lake's note chunks, full-text index and embeddings) and the guard's recognisers read it: resident certificate numbers, both formats (`TW_ARC`); twelve digits, together or in fours joined by a space, dash, dot or slash (the masker leaves three clock times in a row, `TWELVE_DIGITS`; the guard takes every one, `TWELVE_DIGITS_ANY`); bed numbers after their word or written against 床, never a count of beds (`BED_LABELLED`, `BED_NUMBER`); room numbers after or before their word, never a count (`ROOM`, `ROOM_NUMBER`). The guard also reads every span the masker finds (`note_spans`), so it never lets through what the lake's notes hide. Every look ahead is bounded: a note can be one long line.
2. Names without a listed surname: a staff name beside a role word (the roles of a ward's staff), after it (2 to 4 characters, a list of names too, to the end of the name or a verb of a note) or before it (1 to 3 after a boundary), and a name before an honorific or an elder's kinship word (`STAFF_NAME_AFTER`, `STAFF_NAME_BEFORE`, `NAME_HONORIFIC`); never a role's own qualifier, a department, a pronoun, a count, a label word or a common verb or noun of a note.
3. Labelled values as records write them: letters of either case and digits, full-width too, groups joined by `- _ / .` or a space before digits (`_LABELLED_VALUE`, for chart-number and ID labels, `ID_LABELLED` adding the resident certificate's labels), and a labelled phone with spaces, dots, brackets and an extension (`PHONE_LABELLED`).
4. `NOTE_MASK_VERSION` is 5 (4 at the first landing; 5 with the full addresses that hold spaces), so a lake built under an older masking is rebuilt; the goldens are unchanged (the synthetic notes hold none of these shapes). A full Taiwanese address may hold spaces between its parts (`公正路 88 號`, `5F`). Speed: each pattern has a gate that is a necessary condition of it (a word or a character it cannot match without: a pattern the note gives no reason to run is not run), and a pattern that ends at, or starts with, a fixed word (an address's 號, a born word, a role word, an honorific, 床, a chart-number label) runs only in a window around each such word, to the line's end. A test compares the spans with and without both on generated notes and both note layouts. On synthetic notes the masker is no slower than version 3 where every gate opens and about 40% faster where few do.
5. Tests: `tests/unit/test_phi_note_cgrd_shapes.py` (132; every shape inside a discharge summary's and an admission note's layout; synthetic text only, identifiers built at run time): each shape masked by the masker and redacted by the guard, the words of a note left alone, the guard finding every span the masker finds and every twelve digits, a one-line note of 30,000 characters masked in time, the version. A fresh read-only review found a look ahead that made a long line slow (6.6 s for 30,000 characters), the guard losing twelve digits shaped like times, names in lists, after a verb, in brackets, with compound surnames or other roles, more bed, room and labelled forms, and words of a note masked; each is fixed and in the tests.
