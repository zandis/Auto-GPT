# D-504 The integrator may relay the owner's yes on advised choices

- **Task:** an owner's ruling, numbered by the integrator and relayed by the integrator
- **Date:** 2026-11-15
- **Status:** accepted (the owner's ruling; recorded here on 2026-11-16)

## Ruling

1. The integrator may relay the owner's yes on choices the integrator advised.
2. It does not cover amending the owner's own rulings.

## Where the wording comes from

The integrator's session.

## Used by

D-793 and D-887 cite it.
