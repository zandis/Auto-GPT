# D-876 An opted-out patient's OMOP before-images are blanked at retention (D-531)

- **Task:** the owner's ruling D-531 (replaces D-526 for the event table), lane C2. `omop.tb_record_event` kept an opted-out patient's before-images, and `tb_record.extra` the FHIR fields a record carried, for ever. · **Date:** 2026-11-16
- **Decision:** the opt-out worker's `omop` clear blanks them at retention: the lake's month-end months (`settings.retention.month_end_snapshots_months`, 36 when unset), counted as the lake counts (from the first of the month the patient was withheld). An event loses its OMOP image, its sha and a steward's free-text reason, and its record image loses `extra` and is marked blanked; a record keeps its ids and provenance (D-441, a keep entry now) and loses `extra`. Until then the images stay hidden: the export as of a time keeps a withheld patient out. The consent statements the withhold keeps are not blanked. After a lift, an export as of an instant before a blanked image is refused, never guessed.
- **Left:** the clock is `tb_withheld.since`, which a lift and a new opt-out restart; the images stay owed until retention (as D-513's lake files); `omop.tb_conflict` stays owed (D-867).
- **Held by:** `tests/unit/test_the_before_images_go_at_retention.py`, synthetic data only (failing output on the previous code kept: `d531-fail.txt`); the owed list and the registry's `omop.tb_record` entry moved on purpose; the adversarial review's two findings (the kept consents, a refused wind-back) are in.
