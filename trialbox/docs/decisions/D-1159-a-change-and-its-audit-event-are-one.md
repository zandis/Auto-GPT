# D-1159 A change and its audit event are one: a change whose event cannot be written is not kept

- **Task:** the register's open Highs for the console, card AUDIT-ORDER (posted on #639): V3-18 (a break-glass grant stored before its event), V8-04 (an account made, reset, scoped, disabled or enabled before its event) and V8-07's order (the IRB record saved before its event), sent by the integrator on 2026-11-19. · **Date:** 2026-11-19
- **Decision:** the stores take the caller's audit append as `before_commit` and run it inside their own transaction, as the opt-out register and the not-matched check already did (D-273): `BreakGlassStore.open`, `end` and `review`; `UserStore.create` (now with the scope in the same transaction), `set_scope`, `set_password`, `disable`, `enable`, `reset_totp`, `issue_enrol_code` and `open_by_code`; `TeamStore.save_irb`. When the append fails (AuditFull, AuditAnchorBroken), the transaction rolls back and nothing is kept. The pages' events and the account rules' (D-719) moved into those hooks. Every route the audit walk listed as "change first" is now "one transaction". The guarantee is one way: never a change without its event; an event whose change then fails to commit (a database writer's event is durable at once) can stand, the safe side. A second person's confirm whose change rolls back fails its request instead of leaving it "confirmed" (the adversarial review's MED).
- **Left:** the IRB record's event names the fields as saved (before: as read back after a consent-form version was added to the list); V8-07 is closed when this and D-1158 (its typed reason) are both on master.
- **Held by:** `tests/console/test_a_change_whose_audit_fails_is_not_kept.py` (eight cases, the audit volume read as full), and the pinned tests, moved: the strict xfails on V3-18 (`test_asvs_evidence.py`) and V8-04 (`test_audit_trail_routes.py`) are removed and they pass.
