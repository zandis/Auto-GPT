- **Withheld until retention:** the lake's kept snapshots (`lake:ndjson`, `lake:ndjson-rerun`, `lake:db`, `lake:parquet`), the job outputs and the attachments are `keep` entries of the erasure registry under this record. Every job's patient scope drops an opted-out patient at receipt and at publish (D-441), the console's downloads withhold a file that names one (D-860), and the files go when retention rewrites them (365 days for outputs, 36 month-ends for snapshots).
- **The equivalence sample:** the criteria compiler's stored report (the draft's `tests/equivalence.json`, kept in the criteria library's history) names each disagreeing patient by a case label (`case-001`), never the pseudonym, also inside an evidence string. The run keeps the pseudonyms in memory, and nothing reads a stored disagreement's patient back. The compiler's role cannot read the register, so the history keeps nothing an opt-out must remove.
- **The embed cache:** a new clear of the opt-out worker, `embed_cache`, asks the lake service (`POST /cache/prune`) to remove from `embed_cache.duckdb` the vectors of the chunk texts that only the opted-out patients' notes hold in the current snapshot. A text another patient's note holds keeps its vector; one an older kept snapshot still needs is embedded again on demand. Its verify is the same question as a dry run. The owed list goes from 49 to 42.
