# D-875 The screening log keeps its rows, not the patient (ERASE-A2)

- **Task:** ERASE-A2, lane C2's backlog, on the owner's ruling D-532. A never-consented patient's pseudonym stayed in the screening log's five tables after an opt-out (`site.screening_no`, `site.weekly_seen`, `site.pi_marks`, `orch.candidates`, `orch.candidate_events`); a plain delete would break D-762's numbered row and give the number again. · **Date:** 2026-11-16
- **Decision:** a new opt-out clear, `screening_log`, keeps every row and its number and gives a never-consented patient's rows one random token per study (`erased:<the site's day of the opt-out>:<random>`); the PI's note and the registry's reasons go. A study is kept for the patient when any source shows consent (the registry now or ever, the lifecycle, the consent forms, REDCap's read-back or a number not read back yet while the link is on, any EDC draft), over the patient's merged charts. Numbers are compared as the lifecycle keys them (upper case). A person's merged charts are kept or tokened together, each chart with a token of its own (a study numbers each chart once). An open candidate waits for its withdrawal, and a consent recorded after the plan keeps the rows (asked again under a lock). The adversarial review added the lifecycle's subject of the number (`datacore.subject.pid` takes the same token, or the kept number would join the token back to the patient and the lifecycle refresh would put the patient back on the log); the lifecycle never mirrors an erased subject, and the registry's export and the console's search leave token rows out. A source that exists and cannot be read stops the clear with nothing changed. The console counts a token as opted out, so every reader that leaves the opted-out out leaves the row out, and the log shows the day the token carries.
- **Left:** `study.rc_record`, `study.rc_write` and `edc.draft` follow the same rule on a later card (D-532); until then REDCap's read-back still joins a kept number to the pseudonym. A failure between the stores' commits leaves the rest to the next tick with a token of its own. The token's day is D-762's, shown on the log.
