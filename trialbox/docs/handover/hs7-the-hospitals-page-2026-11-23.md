# HS-7: the hospital's page (2026-11-23)

**Reader:** the people who build TrialBox: lane H, lane K, the integrator and the reviewer. Findings for builders,
so in one language.

Card HS-7 of the hospital-simple-install design (`docs/plan/design/hospital-simple-install.md`, section 5 row 7;
sections 2.1, 2.3, 2.5, 2.6, 2.7). Findings in the register's `--add-from` form:
`hs7-the-hospitals-page-2026-11-23/findings.json`. A docs card: no product code changed.

## The page

`docs/handover/hospital-install.en.md`, `.ja.md` and `.zh-TW.md`. The reader is hospital IT, then the box owner.

- **What you need:** the machine prepared by the vendor, the stick, the delivery sheet, an administrator's login,
  500 GB free (`docs/runbook/27-workstation-rtx-pro-6000.md` line 11, the only disk figure the runbook gives for a box; card H-95 changes it),
  and the box owner for the second part.
- **Hospital IT, 7 steps:** plug in, type the delivery sheet's line (the integrator's ruling, comments 7090648193
  and 7090760988 on #639: the first step checks the launcher with stock tools), compare the launcher's code, compare
  the vendor key's fingerprint, confirm the machine, wait, hand the address and code to the owner.
- **The box owner, 6 steps:** open the address, type the code, answer the page, the passphrase only without a TPM,
  "Make my accounts and finish", sign in.
- **When the screen stops:** 14 rows. Each quotes the installer's real line (the HS-1 launcher, `deploy/bootstrap.sh`,
  `deploy/box-install.sh`) and gives one action.

The page names one command (the sheet's line, in a fenced block) and nothing else to type: no flag, no setting, no
path, no card or decision number. The console's words are the catalogue's (`services/console/i18n_firstrun.py`:
所有者 / 負責人, パスフレーズ / 密語, 「アカウントを作成して完了」 / 「建立我的帳號並完成」, 本体の画面に表示されたコード /
本機螢幕上的代碼); "box" and "hospital IT" follow the handover pages and the preflight catalogue (主機, 醫院資訊人員,
病院のIT担当者).

**Steps a hospital does, before and after.** Before: RUNBOOK §1 (its reader was "Aimedics and hospital IT"): the
tier choice, the one-command paragraph and 10 numbered steps, with 31 commands in backticks to type or read and three
files written by hand, then §12 and §5 for TLS, sign-in and backups. After: 13 steps of one action each (7 IT,
6 owner), one line typed, no file edited.

## Where the vendor's and builders' steps went

Nothing was deleted. RUNBOOK §1 (`docs/runbook/01-install-a-new-site.md`) keeps every step and is now the vendor's
page: its reader line names Aimedics and sends a hospital to its page, and its row in the RUNBOOK's section table
says "Aimedics". The bare-machine preparation stays in RUNBOOK §16 (the builders' page), §12 and §5 stay as they are
(their steps are the installer's or the vendor's), and the RUNBOOK's opening sends a hospital to the new page. The
design's rows 1, 3, 4, 5, 30 to 33, 35, 39 to 42, 44 and 46 are off the hospital's page.

## The test

`tests/unit/test_hs7_hospital_page.py` (10 tests): one shell command per page, the same line in the three languages and
exactly the sheet's line `tools/make_bundle.sh` prints;
no setting name, flag, path or internal number outside it (the design's test); every quoted screen line is printed by
`deploy/launcher.sh`, `deploy/bootstrap.sh` or `deploy/box-install.sh`; the three languages have the same headings,
steps, bullets, rows and quotes in the same order; the runbook sends a hospital to the page. Five mutations of the
ja page (a step merged, a flag, a quote changed, a bullet dropped, a setting name) each fail it.
