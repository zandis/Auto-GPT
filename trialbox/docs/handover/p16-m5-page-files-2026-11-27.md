## 1. What was wrong

`console.app.MODULE_PAGES` declares six page files. Three of them, `health_page`, `data_step` and `support_page`,
were never written on any branch. `_module_page` logged "declared page file not in this tree" and the three pages
were off on every box. F0's test let it pass because the three were listed in its `PENDING` set.

## 2. What was built

The pages follow plan 10's rows 10K-6 and 10K-16. Each is for the administrator only: reading needs `ops.view`.
Anyone else gets 403 and a `console.denied` line.

- **The health page** (`/admin/health`, with its twin `/api/v1/admin/health`). One line per fact, green or red,
  and each line has a red case:
  - the last good night per source: red with none, or after 26 hours, so a feed that stopped is red the next
    morning;
  - the newest night's rows: red when it was held or failed;
  - the jobs waiting and the oldest: red after 6 hours;
  - the audit drain's lag: red after 15 minutes, or when nothing has been drained;
  - seconds per model call: red above 30.
- **The data step** (`/admin/data-step`, with its twin). It shows the newest SS-MIX2 night: whether its rows went
  in, the counts per type with their total, and the coverage lines from the night's site profile summary.
  - The console's audit reader keeps only an ingest's total, so the page reads the night's events from the chain
    itself.
  - It keeps counts and type names, and nothing else (P16-03).

rc1's guard allows the two pages by name (`ALLOWED`). They hold counts and ages, never a patient. Its whole-route
test checks that each allowed line names a route the console has.

## 3. Tests

`tests/console/test_m5_health_data_p16.py`, all on fixed clocks.
- Each health line has its red case and its green case.
- The page is read in three languages, and its twin agrees with it.
- A role other than the administrator is refused both pages.
- The synthetic Japanese hospital's night gives its counts per type on the page; a held night leaves the step not
  done.
