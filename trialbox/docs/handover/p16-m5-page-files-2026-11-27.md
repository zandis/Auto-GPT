**Reader:** the people who build TrialBox: the integrator and the lane H lead. Findings for builders.

- **Card:** P-16, the integrator's R1+1 batch D, part H (7210967559, PR #1504). Base: `int/r1plus1` (c3ba5fc4).
- **Date:** 2026-11-27

- **Files:** the new `services/console/health_page.py`, `support_page.py`, `data_step.py`, `i18n_m5.py` and their

- **Files outside the card:** `services/console/rc1_guard.py` (two lines added to `ALLOWED`).

## 1. What was wrong

`console.app.MODULE_PAGES` declares six page files. Three of them, `health_page`, `data_step` and `support_page`,
were never written on any branch. `_module_page` logged "declared page file not in this tree" and the three pages
were off on every box. F0's test let it pass because the three were listed in its `PENDING` set.

## 2. What was built

The three pages follow plan 10's rows 10K-6, 10K-15 and 10K-16. Each is for the administrator only: reading needs
`ops.view`, turning the support session on or off needs `ops.update_apply`. Anyone else gets 403 and a
`console.denied` line.

- **The health page** (`/admin/health`, with its twin `/api/v1/admin/health`). One line per fact, green or red,
  and each line has a red case:
  - the last good night per source: red with none, or after 26 hours, so a feed that stopped is red the next
    morning;
  - the newest night's rows: red when it was held or failed;
  - the jobs waiting and the oldest: red after 6 hours;
  - the audit drain's lag: red after 15 minutes, or when nothing has been drained;
  - seconds per model call: red above 30.
- **The support session** (`/admin/support`), for decision 99. While it is on, every route that rc1's guard
  refuses on a refused box is refused for every role, with the support session's own message and a `console.denied`
  line (`refusal: support_session`).
  - `SupportGuard` is rc1's guard with its question changed, so both refuse the same routes.
  - Start and end are audited as `console.support_session`. The session ends by itself 2 hours after it starts.
  - The state is one file, written to a new file, fsynced and renamed into place.
  - A start writes the state with its event owed, then sends the event. A stop between the two keeps the pages
    closed, and the next read sends the event. A state that cannot be read keeps the pages closed.
- **The data step** (`/admin/data-step`, with its twin). It shows the newest SS-MIX2 night: whether its rows went
  in, the counts per type with their total, and the coverage lines from the night's site profile summary.
  - The console's audit reader keeps only an ingest's total, so the page reads the night's events from the chain
    itself.
  - It keeps counts and type names, and nothing else (P16-03).

rc1's guard allows the three pages by name (`ALLOWED`). They hold counts, ages and a switch, never a patient. Its
whole-route test checks that each allowed line names a route the console has.

## 3. Tests

`tests/console/test_m5_pages_p16.py`: 19 tests, all on fixed clocks.
- Each health line has its red case and its green case.
- The page is read in three languages, and its twin agrees with it.
- A role other than the administrator is refused all three pages.
- During a support session, a coordinator's home page and a patient file are refused. After the end they open
  again, and the audit holds exactly one start and one end.
- The session ends by itself at 2 hours, once.
- A stop between the state and its event is covered, and so is a state that cannot be read.
- The synthetic Japanese hospital's night gives its counts per type on the page.

On the base, 8 of these tests fail, and F0's declared-file test fails once PENDING is emptied.

## 4. Not done, not verified

- No box with a real feed, drain or model server was used. The drain and latency lines were checked on stand-ins
  only.
- The admin page has no link to the three pages yet. Each is reached by its address.
