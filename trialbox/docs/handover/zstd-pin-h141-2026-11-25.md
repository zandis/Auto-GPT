## Why

The base ships `libzstd1` and `zstd` at 1.5.7+dfsg-1. With today's scanner database (downloaded fresh by the card)
that version carries CVE-2026-124851, fixed in trixie-security 1.5.7+dfsg-1+deb13u1 (DSA-6657-1). `db.Dockerfile`
installs exact versions and does not upgrade, so the image kept the base's version, as it did for `zlib1g` (SC-1b,
D-993) and the krb5 libraries (H-93).
