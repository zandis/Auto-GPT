# TrialBox PoC: tester guide

Two pages for the tester. Also in [Japanese](guide.ja.md) and [Traditional Chinese](guide.zh-TW.md).

**The rule: every patient in TrialBox's demonstration is synthetic. Never type, paste, upload or describe anything
from a real patient, anywhere, including the feedback.**

## What you are doing

You try TrialBox, a screening aid, on a demonstration site with made-up patients, in your own language. You do a
clinic day's screening work as a PI, as a sub-investigator and as a coordinator, and tell us what is wrong,
confusing or missing. It is product evaluation, not research on you, and nothing you do here touches a patient or a
trial. The results come from a test model with fixed answers; the pages say so.

## The link and your accounts

The link, your three account names (PI, sub-investigator, CRC), a first password for each and an enrolment code for
each come to you separately, never by the same message as this guide. Use a current browser on a PC; a phone works
too. You need an authenticator app on your phone (any app that reads a TOTP key).

## Signing in the first time (once per account)

1. Open the link and pass the front door with your own mail address.
2. Sign in with the account name and the first password.
3. Set up the second factor: add the key on the screen to your authenticator app, then type the enrolment code and
   the six-digit code from the app.
4. Choose your own password (at least 12 characters). You are signed out: sign in again with it and a new code.
5. Read the acknowledgement, tick the five statements (the two optional ones are your choice) and press
   "I accept".
6. You land on your study's list. The banner on top says the data is synthetic and the tool is not for clinical use.

After this, each sign-in is the password and a code from the app. The console signs you out after 10 minutes without
use.

## Your sessions

| Session | About | You do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | 80 minutes, on a video call | the PI tasks, then the sub-investigator tasks of your script |
| B | 80 minutes, on a video call | the CRC tasks, a short questionnaire, a talk about what you saw |

Your script lists each task with the result you should see: Japan site [scripts/jp.ja.md](scripts/jp.ja.md), Taiwan
site [scripts/tw.zh-TW.md](scripts/tw.zh-TW.md) (in English: [jp.en.md](scripts/jp.en.md),
[tw.en.md](scripts/tw.en.md)). Think aloud as you work. The facilitator watches and helps only if you ask; asking is
fine and tells us something. Between sessions you may use the demo on your own.

## Giving feedback

- After each task the facilitator asks "How easy was this?" (1 to 7).
- Say anything that is wrong, confusing, slow or missing, and how much it matters: would cause a wrong patient
  decision, blocks my task, slows me down, or cosmetic.
- A wrong word in Japanese or Chinese is a finding too.
- Refer to a patient only by the pseudonym on the screen (for example "patient 92ab1972").
- Your feedback is filed under a code, not your name.
- At any time, alone or in a session: "Feedback on this page" at the foot of every page (a flag, a rating, a
  comment). It records the page for you; no patient details in the comment.

## If something goes wrong

- The demo is down: tell the facilitator; after 15 minutes we rebook.
- You typed something from a real patient by mistake: tell the project owner the same day. We delete it and tell you
  what was done. Follow your hospital's own reporting rule as well.
- You want your data shown, corrected or deleted, or you want to stop: write to the project owner, no reason needed.
