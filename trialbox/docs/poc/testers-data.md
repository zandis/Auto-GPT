# Notice on the testers' own data

One notice in three languages: [English](#english), [日本語](#日本語), [繁體中文](#繁體中文). It expands point 5 to
point 7 of the data-handling statement ([data-handling.en.md](data-handling.en.md)). It is about you, the tester:
the demonstration holds no patient data (D-332).

## English

| What | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Who holds it | Aimedics, for the TrialBox PoC. Contact: the project owner, by the mail address given with your accounts. |
| What we keep | Your name, role, institution and mail address; your account names (one per role), the language you chose and the end date of the accounts; the authenticator you set up (a secret key, never shown again); your sign-ins and actions in the console (time, page, action, as audit events, among them the acknowledgement `demo.ack`); the feedback you give (the session record, the questionnaire, what you say in the calls, and a screen recording only if you agreed for that session). |
| What we do not keep | Your password in readable form (only a hash); anything about patients (there are none); recordings you did not agree to. |
| Why | To run the test, to keep the service secure, and to improve the product. Nothing else: no marketing, no sale, no sharing. |
| Where | On one server in Taiwan (Google Cloud, region `asia-east1`) during the PoC; the facilitator's notes and the triaged findings in the project's own files, under a tester code (T1, T2), never your name. |
| How long | Accounts, logs and snapshots: deleted 90 days after the PoC ends at the latest; the host is destroyed after the exit review. Recordings: deleted after the exit review. Feedback: kept, without your name. |
| Who sees it | The project owner and the people building TrialBox for this test. Findings that reach the repository carry the tester code only. |
| Your rights | Ask to see, correct or delete your data, or stop, at any time and without a reason. Withdraw the optional recording or quote consent at any time. Write to the project owner; we answer within a week. |
| If you entered patient information by mistake | Tell the project owner the same day; see point 8 of the statement. |
