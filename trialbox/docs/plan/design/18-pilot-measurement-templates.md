## 1. Purpose and how the templates are used

The pilot claims that TrialBox reduces the clinical load (N-17, NFR-USAB-06). That claim needs a baseline measured the same way as the pilot (TB-DS-04 §8.3 E6, E9, E11) and site facts the design currently assumes (TB-REV-006c §3.2). This document gives the instruments. Each is short on purpose: a CRC with five studies cannot fill in a long form.

| Instrument | When | Who fills it | Time per person | Plan item |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| §2 Self-log | Baseline: 2027-01-11 to 01-22 at both sites (outside 年末年始 and its recovery week; before Lunar New Year). If a site's go-live moves, the two normal working weeks before that go-live; the baseline always ends before that site's Phase 1. Pilot: one week in Phase 1 (week 4; S11 covers only what is installed at go-live per TB-DS-00 §2) and one week in Phase 2 (week 10) | 2 CRCs + 2 PIs per site | ≈ 10 min per working day | TB-PLAN-01 WP-00, MS-05b |
| §2.3 Observation sheet | One clinic half-day per site in the baseline window, and one in Phase 2 | Observer from the pilot team | 4 h observer | WP-00 |
| §3 NASA-TLX (raw) and SUS | NASA-TLX: baseline, week 6, week 12. SUS: weeks 4/6 and 8/12 | Same people | 3 min each | TB-DS-04 §3.7, E9 |
| §4 Usability script | Formative round 1 (S20, S21, list mail) by 2026-12-18 (MS-05c); round 2 (S16, S8, S18, S19) before pilot week 7 (MS-05d); summative (release candidate); at go-live PQ-07 runs T3, T4, T5, T8, T9, T10, T13 and the first half of T2; T1, the second half of T2, T6, T7, T11 and T12 run at the week-6 readiness check (TB-DS-15 PQ-06a) | Moderator (Japan team member in Japanese; CGMH research-office contact in Mandarin) and note-taker, with 3 CRCs, 2 PIs, 1 research-office member per site (TB-PLAN-01 WP-00b) | 60 min per participant | R-03; TB-DS-04 §3.7 |
| §5 Interview guides | Nov–Dec 2026, before feature freeze (MS-05a) | Product owner + engineer | 45–60 min each | R-02, R-05, R-08, R-09 |
| §6 Screening-log columns | From go-live | Generated by TrialBox | none | FR-SCR-10a, FR-RC-15 |

Results are **descriptive**. With about four people per role per site the numbers are reported per person, before and after, with no statistical test (TB-REV-006c C-25). The self-log holds no patient identifiers.

Ethics and management: observing or logging staff time is staff data, not patient data. The site PI asks the department head for agreement before the baseline, and notes the activity to the ethics office where the hospital requires it (TB-PLAN-01 WP-00).

### 2.3 Observation sheet (clinic half-day)

The observer sits in the clinic area (not in the consultation room), records for each study-related event: time, who, task code, duration, whether the patient was present, and the hand-over path (PI → CRC by phone, by note, in person). The observer never records patient names. One sheet per half-day. The aim is to check the self-log, and to see the real moment of recruitment (TB-REV-006c C-14).

## 3. Raw NASA-TLX and SUS

### 3.1 Raw NASA-TLX (unweighted)

Asked about "your study screening and recruitment work over the last week". Six scales from 0 to 100 in steps of 5: mental demand, physical demand, temporal demand, performance (0 = perfect, 100 = failure), effort, frustration. Raw TLX = mean of the six. Use a validated Japanese translation and a validated traditional-Chinese translation; the site research office confirms which version the hospital already uses, if any. Reported per person: baseline, week 6, week 12. No threshold (TB-DS-04 §3.7).

### 3.2 SUS

The 10-item System Usability Scale, in a validated Japanese and traditional-Chinese translation, scored 0–100. Criterion SUS ≥ 70 (NFR-USAB-09). Given after weeks 4/6 and 8/12.

### 3.3 Notice load (no form; from the system)

From the FR-NOTIF-08 page per week: notices per role, snoozes, opt-outs. Reported as counts: "no more than one PI and one CRC per site snoozing all notices for more than two consecutive weeks" (TB-REV-006c C-26); the pilot team asks those people why.

## 4. Formative usability test script (R-03)

**Setting:** a quiet room at the hospital, the participant's usual device (per TB-DS-15 PR-07: phone, hospital phone or clinic PC), paper or clickable prototypes in Japanese at the Japan head hospital and Mandarin at CGMH, native-speaking moderator, one note-taker. Think-aloud. 60 minutes. No real patient data: the prototypes use the e2e fixture.

**Introduction (read out):** "We are testing the screens, not you. TrialBox suggests patients; it never decides who is eligible. Please say what you are thinking as you go. You can stop at any time."

| # | Task (participant) | Who | Success criterion | Record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T1 | "It is the morning of your clinic day. Open the list you received and tell me which patients you would talk to today." (PI list mail, then S16) | PI | Names the "Your clinic" candidates; finds the other-doctor rows | Time, taps, misreadings |
| T2 | "Mark this patient so the CRC knows to approach them." / "This patient is followed by a colleague who is not on the study. What do you do?" | PI | Approach in ≤ 2 taps; uses "Ask treating doctor" | Taps (including the TOTP step if it appears, BD-17) |
| T3 | "Decide this patient is not suitable for now, with a reason." | PI | Uses "Not suitable", picks a study reason | Whether any label is read as "ineligible" |
| T4 | "Approve the clinical criteria of this study." (S20, two flagged rows) | PI | Opens each flagged row once, approves | Taps; whether code mapping is mistaken as the PI's job |
| T5 | "Confirm the code mapping of this study." (S20, desktop) | CRC / data manager | Confirms, opens amber rows | Errors |
| T6 | "It is 08:30. What do you need to do today?" (CRC digest, then S8 "Today") | CRC | Identifies today's visits and new PI marks | Time |
| T7 | "The doctor has just marked a patient in clinic. Where do you see it?" (S8 "Marks today") | CRC | Finds it without reloading | Time to notice |
| T8 | "You reached the patient; they booked a visit. Record it." / "They declined." | CRC | Each in ≤ 2 taps (FR-SCR-10c) | Taps |
| T9 | "What does 'would need checking after consent: MMSE' mean here?" | CRC | Says it is not to be done before consent | Misreading = critical finding |
| T10 | "Set up this new study: type, team, ethics approval, explainer file, REDCap project link." (S21) | CRC, then PI confirms | Completes; PI confirms the approval record | Time, fields that were unclear |
| T11 | "Turn off notices for next week." (S19) | any | Snooze in ≤ 2 taps | Taps |
| T12 | "Produce this year's numbers for the annual report." (FR-REP-09) | research office | Finds the export | Time; whether the numbers match what they file today |
| T13 | "How many patients might be eligible for this protocol?" (feasibility from criteria) | CRC / PI | Report in ≤ 3 actions | Actions |

**Questions after the tasks:** "Is there anything on these screens you would not trust?" · "Did you see any number you thought was a score?" · "What would you stop doing if you had this?" · "What would this add to your week?" Then SUS.

**Classification of use errors:** critical (would exclude or misattribute a patient, approve without opening a flagged row, or prompt a pre-consent research procedure); serious (task not completed or wrong study); minor (slow, hesitations). Round-1 findings go into TB-DS-09 §11 before feature freeze (MS-05c, MS-08); round-2 findings before the Phase-2 release (MS-05d).

## 5. Site interview guides (R-02, R-05, R-08, R-09)

45–60 minutes each, notes in the site language, no patient data. Answers update TB-DS-17 §1 and §4 and TB-DS-15 prerequisites.

### 5.1 CRC (two per site)

1. Walk me through last week's screening for one study: where did you find candidates, how long did it take, what did you write down where?
2. Where does your screening log live (paper, Excel, REDCap)? Who else reads it? What does the monitor check in it?
3. Who introduces a study to a patient? What happens when the patient's doctor is not on the study team (主治医の了承 / 主治醫師同意)?
4. When the doctor decides in clinic that a patient should hear about a study, how do you find out? How quickly?
5. Which study mails or notices do you get today? Which do you read? What would be too many?
6. What do you type twice today (EMR, Excel, REDCap)?
7. Do you ever check something (a test, a score) before consent? When is that routine care?

### 5.2 PI

1. Which days are your clinic days? How do you remember which patients might fit a study?
2. Where would you look at a candidate list: phone, clinic PC beside the EMR, mail? Is your phone allowed on the hospital network?
3. When you approve study criteria, what do you check yourself and what do you leave to the CRC or the lab?
4. What would make you stop using a list like this?

### 5.3 Research office (治験事務局 / 臨床研究支援センター; CGMH 臨床試驗中心)

1. What happens when a new study starts: which forms, which accounts, who records the ethics approval?
2. What do you file for the annual progress report (実施状況報告 / IRB 期中報告)? Which numbers, by when, from where?
3. How does the committee handle wording changes to a supplementary explanation sheet (補助説明資料 / 受試者說明補充資料): new approval, or approval with conditions? Does it need an ICF amendment?
4. In an industry trial (企業治験 / 廠商委託試驗), who approves patient-facing material and how is it recorded?
5. What may an SMO CRC access?
6. Would you accept a weekly upload of the CRC's log for the pilot comparison (TB-DS-04 §8.9)?
7. Who approaches a candidate in your protocols: only the treating physician, or a study investigator after the treating physician has agreed (主治医の了承 / 主治醫師同意)? Where is that written (BD-19)?
8. Does the committee treat an opt-out registry that lists new members by chart number as needing its approval of that use (BD-20)?

### 5.4 REDCap administrator (R-05)

1. REDCap version and LTS track; API enabled for project-level tokens?
2. For each pilot study project: field names for consent date, enrolment / randomisation, screen-fail and reason, withdrawal, sponsor subject number; events if longitudinal; DAGs.
3. Will you add the text field `tb_screening_no` (BD-18)? Does any project hold the chart number (for `mrn` link mode), and would the site allow its use?
4. Can the appliance reach the REDCap API from its network zone?

### 5.5 IT security contact (R-04, R-06)

1. PI device path: personal phone on Wi-Fi or VPN, hospital phone, or clinic PC only? TOTP app allowed? (TB-DS-15 PR-07)
2. Can a clinic PC show a second browser window beside the EMR?
3. Can the appliance send mail to all staff mailboxes?
4. Appointment (予約 / 掛號) data: which export, how often?

## 6. TB-TMPL-03 Screening-log columns (ICH E6(R3))

The screening log TrialBox keeps per study (FR-SCR-10a) and exports (FR-RC-15, XLSX inside the box, CSV in REDCap import format) has these columns, as in TB-DS-07 §4.1. It is meant to be usable as the site's screening log, so that the CRC does not keep a second one.

| # | Column | Source | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `screening_no` | Issued by TrialBox at the first PI mark of Approach (BD-18, FR-STUDY-08), or the site's own sequence started on S21 | The same number goes into REDCap field `tb_screening_no` |
| 2 | Patient | Pseudonym; chart number only after an audited reveal, only inside the box (BD-04) | Never in mail |
| 3 | Date first listed | Registry event | |
| 4 | Date screened | First pre-screen or PI review date | |
| 5 | Contact outcomes with dates | Coordinator events (FR-SCR-10c): reached – visit booked; reached – declined; not reached (retry date); not approached (physician decision); lost contact | Each with date |
| 6 | Status | identified / pre-screened / contacted / consented / screen-failed / enrolled / withdrawn (TB-DS-07 outcome → status table) | REDCap wins for consent, enrolment, screen-fail and withdrawal where linked |
| 7 | Reason category + text | declined; not approached (physician decision, per-study reason); lost contact; screen-failed on criterion (REDCap choice mapped); withdrawal reason | A declined patient is never reported as failing a criterion |
| 8 | Consent date | REDCap `consent_field` via the project link, or entered on the row when the study is not linked | |
| 9 | Enrolment date | REDCap `enrol_field` | |
| 10 | Withdrawal date | REDCap `withdraw_field` | |
| (11) | Sponsor subject number | REDCap `subject_no_field`, where the project holds it | Optional |
| (12) | REDCap `record_id` | From the link | Present when linked |

The annual research-office export (FR-REP-09) counts rows of this log per status and year (k = 5 inside the site).
