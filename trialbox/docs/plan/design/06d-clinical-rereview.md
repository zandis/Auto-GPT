## 0. Summary and overall score

**Overall clinical score: 4.1 / 5** (unweighted mean of the ten criteria in §1 = 4.07; not rounded up). Up from **3.4** in TB-REV-006c. The target of 4.5 is **not reached on paper**, and §4 says plainly what the remaining points need.

The fix pass did what TB-REV-006c §3.1 asked for, and did it in the right places:

- **The clinical layer is now traced end to end.** TB-PLAN-01 §3.1a carries WP-00 and WP-17..WP-20/19b with hours and a go-live split; TB-DS-02 has runtime views §6.13–6.15 and the stores in §8.16; TB-DS-08 has ICD-13 (notices) and ICD-14 (uploads); TB-DS-04 §6.2 traces every FR-STUDY, FR-NOTIF, FR-UI-14..19, FR-REP-06..09, FR-SCR-07b/07c/10c, FR-CON-09/10, FR-RC-15/16, FR-AUTH-13 and NFR-USAB-06..10 row to a suite, and §3.5 walks S8/S16/S17/S19/S20/S21. The capacity ratio is restated honestly (≈ 620 h to go-live against ≈ 400 h). (C-02 closed.)
- **The pilot is now two phases everywhere it matters** (BD-16; TB-DS-17 §0, §2.1, §2.2; TB-DS-04 §8.2; TB-TMPL-01 §1, §2, §5; TB-DS-09 P8; TB-DS-11 H-01 (h)), with a Phase-1 added-time table, chart review as a funded pilot line, and the comparison log imported by the pilot team. (C-01, C-04, C-21 closed in substance.)
- **The baseline has a work package, an instrument, an owner, a window and a prerequisite** (WP-00, MS-05b, TB-TMPL-07 §2–3, TB-DS-15 PR-10, TB-TMPL-01 F2). (C-03 closed.)
- **One consent-explainer process** (BD-15 v0.5) is now written the same way in TB-TMPL-01 §6, TB-DS-09 S9/S21, TB-DS-02 §6.7, TB-DS-11 H-11, TB-DS-15 OQ-06a, TB-DS-08 §6 and FR-CON-06/09/10, with the committee-edit path and the sponsor step. - **The CRC flow has its missing pieces**: TrialBox-issued `screening_no` and the `tb_screening_no` link (BD-18, ICD-04 §4.1a, FR-RC-16), the contact-outcome → status mapping table (TB-DS-07 §4.1), S21 study set-up with AM-01 rows and a store (TB-DS-09 S21, FR-STUDY-07, TB-DS-07 §4.6.1), the "Marks today" pane (FR-UI-18), the two-sign-off S20, one mark vocabulary, no scores for clinicians, the research-office persona and FR-REP-09. (C-06..C-18 closed.)

Why it is 4.1 and not 4.5:

1. **Three criteria are still capped by facts no document can supply** (workload realism, site-practice fit, measurability): every minute is (E), no CRC or PI has used the new screens, the device policy, REDCap field names and committee behaviour are unverified. TB-REV-006c §3.3 said this; it is still true. §4 gives the path.
2. **The fixes introduced release-line inconsistencies that a site would trip over at go-live** (D-02, High): TB-PLAN-01 puts the notification service (WP-18) at 0 h for go-live and the role homes on the February–March line, while TB-DS-15 PQ-04 and PQ-07, TB-DS-17 §2.2 (Phase-1 rows) and TB-TMPL-07 §1 and §4 assume the clinic-day list, digest, S8 "Today" and "Marks today" exist at PQ. TB-DS-17 §2.2 also tags reconciliation M1 where TB-PLAN-01 WP-19b says M2 early.
3. **Stale wording survives in the documents a site signs**: TB-DS-01 §1.2 still defines the pilot as compare-only shadow mode (D-01); TB-DS-03 §5.2–5.3 and TB-DS-04 §2.2 still describe the BD-07 manual release (D-12); the SRS and architecture still tag EDC-lite M2 (D-14).
4. **Four substantive clinical points remain**: the screening number is D4 but is put into e-mail (D-03); the ethics pack tells the committee "only the treating physician" while S21 lets investigators introduce a study after 主治医の了承 (D-04); the flow-B ethics gate skips opt-out registries that still resolve identities (D-20); the formative usability test has an instrument but no hours, owner or milestone (D-06).
5. **The 2027-02-08 date is not yet resourced on paper** (D-05): TB-PLAN-01 §1 assumes 10 h/week from the Japan team, §5 needs 15 h/week of engineering plus ≈ 70 h of drafting moved to them, and nothing counts the moderation of the formative test.

None of D-01..D-20 is hard to fix; §4.1 estimates one to two working days. With them fixed, the paper score is about **4.2–4.3**; **4.3–4.5** needs the site inputs R-02..R-05 and R-07 (late January 2027); **≥ 4.5** needs the Phase-2 measurement (≈ May 2027).

## Annex: method

- Every file in the "reviewed in full" row was read at its exact path on the project mount; the large files were read by the sections named and searched with grep for every C-finding's keywords (RELEASE, BD-07, shadow, Not eligible, 対象外, 100 h / 78 h, formative, every new FR ID, BD-15..18). Nothing in any other design file was changed. The repository was not re-read; built-state claims are taken from TB-REV-006c §2.2 and the documents' own `a1e55fa` statements.
- A C-finding is marked Closed only when the fix appears in the document the finding named *and* no other document in the set contradicts it; a contradiction in any document, revised or not, makes it Partly and produces a D-finding.
- Scores are per criterion against what a site would do with the document as written; the overall score is the unweighted mean, as in TB-REV-006, 006b and 006c. Where a finding rests on practice (how CRCs, PIs and committees work in Japanese and Taiwanese hospitals), it is stated as practice to be confirmed in R-02/R-07, not as regulation; regulatory points cite the same sources the set uses (ICH E6(R3), J-GCP, 生命・医学系指針, HSRA, PDPA) and are not legal advice.
