```
    --pathology-run docs/plan/measurements/poc-1-2026-11-15
```

master `fae5c1f7`), under UTC and under Asia/Taipei (see "The site's zone" below).

**No figure on this page meets a target.** A target is met only by a launch (D-1064 item 4). The replay's figures,
cause-level ones included, decide whether a launch is worth its cost; they are not results. Where the figure lies
inside the target, this page says "within the target" (the report's `within_target`; its `met` is always false).

Each figure names its level. Level **(a)** scores the verdicts as stored, using the current scorer. Level **(b)**
re-runs the parts that need no model, from the stored model replies (D-1111). The corrected key is v2 (D-1104),

## The site's zone (card H-6)

The integrator asked whether launch P1b ran in a zone other than UTC, which could move the replay's age lines by a
day at the edges. The replay now takes the zone from the run's own record when it stores one, else `TB_TZ`, else UTC,
and prints which it used and where it came from (D-1117).

- **The stored run records no zone.** `work/set-clock.json` holds only the time used and the deadline. Each SCREEN
  report's provenance says `"clock": "system clock"`, `run_date` 2026-11-16 and `snapshot` 2026-11-15, with no zone.
  `work/services.log` and `work/raw-traces.json` stamp `+00:00`, but `tb_common.logging` writes UTC whatever the site
  zone is, so that says nothing about it. `built/site-provenance/PROVENANCE.md` names none. The driver kept the box's
  settings (`acc-env-<set>.json`, `acc-settings-*.json`) in the run's logs, outside the stored sets.
- **A hint, not a record.** `work/gateway-audit.json` counts 6,717 judge calls for sigir since `2026-11-16T08:40:28`
  (UTC), while the set's own traces hold 1,014 calls. The review found the cause (D5): the audit stamps the site's
  offset and the start was compared as a string. Such an over-count happens only when the audit's zone is ahead of
  UTC, which fits Asia/Taipei. The stored sets cannot say more.
- **Re-run under both zones.** On the code of this card, with no `TB_TZ` (UTC) and with `TB_TZ=Asia/Taipei`, every
  figure on this page is the same. Every verdict of the level (b) answers is the same, on both keys and both sets.
  That is 3,198 verdicts, 1,124 of them on age lines. No level (b) figure moves and no age-line verdict moves. The
  age tables are built for the run's index day, 2026-11-16, from dates, so a zone has nothing to shift there.

## The conflicted pairs (D-1064)

D-1064 has key v2 mark `conflicted` each pair whose label conflicts with the same physicians' criterion labels; the
pair is never relabelled. The integrator ruled on lane H's question on 2026-11-17 at 05:56 UTC: all the pairs the rule
taken from the gold marks, because a mark in the key must be the same for every run. The rule
(`tools.public_accuracy.key.conflicted`, the scorer's S1 definition) is a pair labelled eligible for which an
inclusion is labelled `not included` or an exclusion `excluded` (D-1118). Over P1b's whole sigir gold it marks 6 of
the 46 eligible pairs, and P1b answered all 6. It leaves unmarked 10 "would consider" pairs that have such a label:
"would consider" does not claim every criterion met, and the target reads eligible pairs only.

| Pair | The criterion label that excludes | P1b's box at (a) | At (b) |

| sigir-201413 x NCT00163709 | inclusion "...over 40 years old...": not included | excluded on "Aged 40 years or older", the line the labels exclude on | the same |
| sigir-20142 x NCT00711399 | exclusion "Respiratory distress": excluded | excluded on "Respiratory distress", the line the labels exclude on | the same |
| sigir-201418 x NCT01260883 | exclusion "Renal or hepatic disease...": excluded | excluded on "Aged 0.17 years or older": **a finding to trace** | review, open on the renal line and 7 others |
| sigir-201410 x NCT02097186 | 4 inclusions, one per kind of surgery: not included | review, open on those 4 lines and 12 others | the same |
| sigir-201425 x NCT00282269 | inclusions "severe traumatic brain injury..." and "mechanically ventilated": not included | review, open on those 2 lines and 11 others | review, open on those 2 and 8 others |
| sigir-201429 x NCT00703417 | inclusion "Postmenopausal female, 55-75 years old": not included | review, open on that line and 19 others | the same |

**The guard.** A conflicted pair that the box excluded on a line the physicians did not label as excluding is listed
as a finding to trace. It is never set aside silently: the report's `conflicted_excluded_on_another_line`, printed
under "Findings to trace". On P1b that is sigir-201418 x NCT01260883 at level (a). The box excluded it on the placed
age line "Aged 0.17 years or older", a fault of the benchmark site (B1 and B2, D-1104), not on the renal line. At level
(b) the line goes to a person (F2, D-946), and the box's answer is review.

The manifest (`eval/public_accuracy_key/MANIFEST.json`, v2, `launch_p1b_rows_that_change.sigir.conflicted`) lists the
6 with the reason D-1064, and `key-diff` lists a mark as a v1 to v2 difference. A v2 build writes them to
`gold/conflicted.json`; the replay's offline v2 of P1b's stored v1 gold marks them by the same rule and checks them
against the manifest (the same 6).
