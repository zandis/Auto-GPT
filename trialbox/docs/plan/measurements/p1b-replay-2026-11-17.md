  jmed rows whose note the run's judge read in full (319 of 594).

## The site's zone (card H-6)

The integrator asked whether launch P1b ran in a zone other than UTC, which could move the replay's age lines by a
day at the edges. The replay now takes the zone from the run's own record when it stores one, else `TB_TZ`, else UTC,
and prints which it used and where it came from (D-1117).

- **The stored run records no zone.** `work/set-clock.json` holds only the time used and the deadline. Each SCREEN
  report's provenance says `"clock": "system clock"`, `run_date` 2026-11-16 and `snapshot` 2026-11-15, with no zone.
  `work/services.log` and `work/raw-traces.json` stamp `+00:00`, but `tb_common.logging` writes UTC whatever the site
  zone is, so that says nothing about it. `built/site-provenance/PROVENANCE.md` names none. The driver kept the box's
  settings (`acc-env-<set>.json`, `acc-settings-*.json`) in the run's logs, outside the stored sets.
- **A hint, not a record.** `work/gateway-audit.json` counts 6,717 judge calls for sigir since `2026-11-16T08:40:28`
  (UTC), while the set's own traces hold 1,014 calls. The review found the cause (D5): the audit stamps the site's
  offset and the start was compared as a string. Such an over-count happens only when the audit's zone is ahead of
  UTC, which fits Asia/Taipei. The stored sets cannot say more.
- **Re-run under both zones.** On the code of this card, with no `TB_TZ` (UTC) and with `TB_TZ=Asia/Taipei`, every
  figure on this page is the same. Every verdict of the level (b) answers is the same, on both keys and both sets.
  That is 3,198 verdicts, 1,124 of them on age lines. No level (b) figure moves and no age-line verdict moves. The
  age tables are built for the run's index day, 2026-11-16, from dates, so a zone has nothing to shift there.
