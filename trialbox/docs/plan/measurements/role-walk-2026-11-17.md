## How a cell is decided

- **Expected** comes from the committed table: `permit(role, action, standing)` for each declared action (all, or any), with the column's standing in GZQO read from the matrix's header; `public` lets anyone in; `signed_in` and `scope` every signed-in account. A route that names GZQO's data refuses a column that does not see GZQO (D-104, D-380). The orchestrator's routes declare their action on the route (`require_actor`).
- **Got:** allowed = 2xx, or a redirect to another page (not to sign-in and not back with `err=`); refused = 401, 403, a redirect to sign-in, or 404 for a column that does not see GZQO on a route of GZQO's data. Anything else (a 4xx validation answer, another 404, a 5xx, a redirect with `err=`) is unclear, never counted. On the orchestrator: refused = 403 from the dependency with reason `right`.
- **Valid bodies** (fresh state per call) for 13 changing routes: `POST /data-ops/batches/{bid}/decide`, `POST /api/v1/data/batches/{bid}/decide`, `POST /breakglass`, `POST /breakglass/{gid}/review`, `POST /site/opt-outs`, `POST /api/v1/site/opt-outs`, `POST /studies/{rs}/patients/{pid}/reveal`, `POST /studies/{rs}/candidates/{pid}/reveal`, `POST /pilot/jobs/{job_id}/reveal`, `POST /studies/{rs}/patients/{pid}/mark`, `POST /ops/users`, `POST /api/v1/studies/{rs}/patients/{pid}/reveal`, `POST /api/v1/studies/{rs}/patients/{pid}/mark`. Every other changing route sends its CSRF token only; where a column passes the check, it stops at validation and the cell is unclear (pinned in `tests/console/data/role_walk_unclear.txt`).
- **The check comes first:** on those routes a refused column is first sent no body; it must be refused then too (6 cells on the two reveal pages ask the purpose or reason before the role, and are listed in the test). A refused cell whose request reached the orchestrator's stand-in counts as refused by the backend, not the console (D-483: the console cannot sign what it would not allow); none was found.
- **Audit:** each request carries a tag; the audit records written while it is served are counted for it.
