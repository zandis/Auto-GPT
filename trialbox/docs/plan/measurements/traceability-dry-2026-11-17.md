`tools/traceability.py` is the repository's convention: a test marks what it proves with
`@pytest.mark.req("<spec v4 id>")`, and the CI step "Traceability from the tests" in
`.github/workflows/trialbox-ci.yml` joins each marked test's result from the shards' `trace.jsonl` and writes a
table, kept as the artifact `traceability`. This tool reuses its Appendix D reader. `tools/gen_traceability.py` is not
changed: run with `--strict` it says "clean", and its committed CSV equals a fresh run.

## Decisions that state a rule and that no test cites

The heuristic, exactly: a decision whose registry row is `merged`, whose text (a file `docs/decisions/D-n-*.md`, or
its paragraph in `docs/DECISIONS.md`) contains one of the words must, never, refuses, is refused, only, and whose
