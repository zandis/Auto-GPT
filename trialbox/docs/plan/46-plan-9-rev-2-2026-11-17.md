## 1. For the owner: one page

### Is plan 9 good enough as a product today? By area

| Area | Verdict | The number that carries it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Coding and terminology** | **Not yet.** The model is asked to choose a code from lists that hold almost nothing, so it mostly chooses none; medicines reach neither a table nor the model | 626 of 676 code-choice calls in launch P2b chose no code (93 %); 4 of 200 medicine names resolved [06, measured] |
| **Validation** | **Not yet.** No validation record has been executed; the nightly that tests the shipped shape has not passed once in the week read; the accuracy target as worded cannot be proved by any finite set | Nightly e2e failed on all 8 runs of 11-11 to 11-17; mutation score 59.3 % (floor 60, target 90) [07, measured] |
| **Serving and performance** | **Good enough for a nightly delta at a mid-size hospital; not yet tuned.** The GPU is mostly idle; the bottleneck is how many questions the product sends at once, and a 117-second CPU gap per protocol | Answers written at about 37 tokens a second; at most 7 % (judge) and 12 % (verifier) of the cache used; a mean 116.6 s between compile and screen per protocol [08, measured] |
| **Interface** | **Not yet.** Text contrast passes and the PI's phone sheet is good, but there is no design system: pages have no common skeleton and the same state is drawn many ways | 30 of 59 first-met pages have no primary button; 60 state classes in 11 families; 1 of 83 form pages warns before typed text is lost [09, measured] |
| **Architecture and quality** | **Good enough to run; not yet to extend by module.** The parts are well made, but 15 service packages import one another in one loop | One import cycle of 15 packages; 22 imports point against the layering; 102 places read the clock [10, measured] |
| **Connectors** | **Ready after named cards** for a Taiwan or Japan site on CSV or SS-MIX2; **not ready** for an Oracle or SQL Server replica or a US site | Lines 1 to 9 of the conformance definition pass only for the hospital CSV export today [read: plan 9 section 4b]; no Oracle or SQL Server driver [01, read] |
| **The EDC** | **Not yet.** Five independent security reviews; each of the first four found ways to reach another site's data or raise one's own rights; round five still found two ways to gain rights. CI does not start; nothing is merged | Round 5: 2 rights-gaining findings; 197 tests pass, locally only [EDC, read and measured by lane J] |

**Why five rounds on the EDC, and did they "find nothing"?** They found a great deal. Each round was a fresh reviewer
given the code, not the earlier conclusions. Rounds 1 to 4 each found ways for a person to read or change another
site's data, or to raise their own rights. After round 3 the method changed: one authorization function that every
route must call, a table test of 1,092 record-level and 901 project-level cells (who × what) run against the live
service, and a "module profile" that ships only the routes TrialBox uses. Round 5 confirmed every earlier hole closed
(it re-ran every earlier probe: all refused), sampled about 70 table cells against the written rules (all matched),
found no stored cross-site scripting, the audit chain verifying and no route that creates a super-administrator; and
it still found **two** new ways to gain rights, plus ordinary hardening. Round 6 (fix, then a fresh review) is
running [EDC, read by lane J]. **Good enough as a product? Not yet** [opinion]. Section 4, band 9m, and criteria 26 to
31 say what must be true.

### The ten changes that matter most, in order

1. **Make the two nightlies green and give a red night an owner the same day**; raise the mutation score per file
   (9i-1 to 9i-3) [07]. Every later claim of "it works" stands on this.
2. **Record what the GPU does** before tuning it: per-call duration, queue wait and tokens in every launch (9j-1), and
   the ladder report (plan 9's 9c-1) [08, 05].
3. **Give the model real code lists, and load all of your Athena download** by streaming into one store: candidates
   from the full code systems, medicines through RxNorm and ATC codes, a replay that measures it with no model (9h-1 to
   9h-4) [06].
4. **Restate "no eligible patient excluded" as a bound per language, frozen and declared before the run**, on a
   clinician-labelled gold set (9i-5 to 9i-9; decisions 17 to 19) [07].
5. **Run G-1, G-2 and G-4 on your yes**, then switch CUDA graphs on and send more questions at once (9g-9, 9j-3)
   [08].
6. **Find and cut the 117 seconds between reading a protocol and screening it** (9j-4, 9j-5) [08].
7. **Finish the EDC's security work**: round 6's fixes, the table test widened to request bodies, "own role" targets
   and concurrent requests, then reviews until two in a row find nothing that gives rights (9m-1 to 9m-8).
8. **Make the architecture rules executable**, then cut the import loop and do the ten cheap hardening changes
   (9l-1, 9l-2, 9l-12, 9l-8; review 10 section 6.2) [10].
9. **One design system**: tokens, one page header with one primary action, one state badge, one form field, and a
   term list read by clinicians (9k-1, 9k-5, 9k-4, 9k-6, 9k-16) [09].
10. **Execute the validation records**: the EDC's IQ/OQ/PQ, `trialbox validate`, a generated traceability matrix
    (9m-11, 9i-14, 9i-15) [07].
