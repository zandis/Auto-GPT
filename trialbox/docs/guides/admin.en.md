# TrialBox guide for the administrator

Made from the help TrialBox shows on each page, so it says what the screens say. Print it for training; Help shows the latest one.

## What you do

You keep the box running: accounts, health, jobs and data sources. You do not approve or mark.

## What TrialBox lets you do

start requests and keep the registry; the research office's yearly counts (small counts hidden); the site's set-up and site facts; the box's REDCap connection and its access key; the hold after a restore: see it and release it; start runs; EDC prefill; operations: backups, updates, logs, network sheet; run a failed nightly job of the box again, with its own inputs; accounts: passwords, disable, enable; break-glass for one patient or one job (60 minutes, reviewed); read break-glass grants; start or end the support session (at most 2 hours; it closes the record pages to every account); which records a model, build or prompt touched (every search recorded); data operations: the nights of every source (the steward decides on a held night)

## Your pages

### Home

Your home: the work for your role, most urgent first.

### Feasibility report

How many patients pass each criterion at this hospital, and how many are expected per month. Numbers under the small-cell limit are shown as a range.

### Reports

Finalised feasibility reports of your studies.

### Administration

The box's health, its jobs (with Cancel and Retry) and its data sources. A red state links here; the runbook says what to do.

### Users

The accounts of this box: add one, reset a password, change its studies or switch it off. An account that sees patients is opened only after a second person confirms.

### Account requests

Account changes that wait for a second person: confirm or refuse each one. Nobody confirms their own request.

### Break-glass

Emergency access to one patient's record: the grants opened, their reasons, and the review each one needs.

### Data operations

The hospital's nightly data: each night that was loaded or held, the tables a check named, and your decision on a held night.

### Runs

Every job the box ran or is running, newest first.

### Your account

Your account: studies in scope, language and password.

### Notices received

The notices TrialBox sent you, newest first.

### Notice settings

When and in which language TrialBox mails you.

### Start a request

Start a job. Nothing runs until you press the button on this page.

### Send in a protocol

Paste or upload criteria to get a feasibility report.

### The command field

What the command field understood, or examples when it did not.

### Site set-up

Site set-up: the three questions, what is still to do, and the lists to check.

### Ask TrialBox

Ask TrialBox: answers about what to do next and why a patient is on the list, with links.

## Commands to try

Type one in the command field at the top of any page; STUDY stands for your study's code.

- `jobs`
- `data sources`
- `system health`
- `users`

## When you need help

Ask your site's TrialBox administrator; Help on any page gives the address.
