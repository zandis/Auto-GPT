# TrialBox guide for the study coordinator

Made from the help TrialBox shows on each page, so it says what the screens say. Print it for training; Help shows the latest one.

## What you do

You set up studies, start feasibility and screening, and record contact outcomes.

## What TrialBox lets you do

start requests and keep the registry; patient-level rows and patient checks; show chart numbers (recorded); the research office's yearly counts (small counts hidden); the study tasks of the delegation log; change a study's state and identifiers; a study's set-up, data sources and forms; the site's set-up and site facts; start runs; EDC prefill (where the study's delegation log allows); copy-assist: the signed values to type into the sponsor's EDC, and their CSV file (where the study's delegation log allows); copy-assist: tick values as copied into the sponsor's EDC (where the study's delegation log allows); the SAE register; patient record summary of a study patient (read, every read logged); the monthly check of the delegation log; record the treating doctor's answer; record a study's IRB approval and consent-form version; pseudonyms in the audit trail; send a criteria-not-matched patient back to the weekly list

## Your pages

### Home

Your home: the work for your role, most urgent first.

### This week's patients

The patients who may fit, with their next visit. Open a patient to see why and mark Approach, Need info or Not suitable now.

### The patient sheet

One patient for one study: each criterion with its evidence. The chart number appears only after you ask for it, and that is recorded.

### Feasibility report

How many patients pass each criterion at this hospital, and how many are expected per month. Numbers under the small-cell limit are shown as a range.

### Screening log

Everyone approached for this study, by screening number, with what happened next. Status from REDCap is read back each night.

### Cohort

Members of the registry or observational study. There is no contact step.

### Study set-up

The study's settings: type, REDCap link and screening-log options. The coordinator fills it in.

### Candidates

The patients across the hospital who may fit this study. Open one to see each criterion and ask the treating doctor.

### Referrals

Every request to a treating doctor for this study, and the doctor's answer. Approach waits until the doctor agrees.

### EDC prefill

Values TrialBox drafted for REDCap from the hospital record. Check each one against its source, sign, then send.

### Reports

Finalised feasibility reports of your studies.

### Staff register

The hospital staff who may be named on a study's delegation log, with their department and contact.

### Runs (where your account and the study's delegation log allow)

Every job the box ran or is running, newest first.

### Your account

Your account: studies in scope, language and password.

### Notices received

The notices TrialBox sent you, newest first.

### Notice settings

When and in which language TrialBox mails you.

### Registry

The candidate registry: where the study team is with each pseudonymous patient.

### Start a request

Start a job. Nothing runs until you press the button on this page.

### Send in a protocol

Paste or upload criteria to get a feasibility report.

### The command field

What the command field understood, or examples when it did not.

### Site set-up

Site set-up: the three questions, what is still to do, and the lists to check.

### Ask TrialBox

Ask TrialBox: answers about what to do next and why a patient is on the list, with links.

## Words you will see

- Participant: a patient with a screening number of the study, from candidate to the end.
- Screen failure: a candidate who will not be enrolled (not eligible, declined or not reached).
- Visit window: the days before and after a visit's due day on which the protocol allows the visit.
- Overdue: the window has closed and the visit is not recorded as done or missed.
- Adverse event: any unwanted medical event during the study, written as the doctor wrote it. TrialBox never grades it.
- Serious: death, life-threatening, a hospital stay, a disability, a congenital anomaly or another important medical event.
- Deviation: something done differently from the protocol (a visit outside its window, a missed test).

## Commands to try

Type one in the command field at the top of any page; STUDY stands for your study's code.

- `FEAS STUDY`
- `SCREEN STUDY`
- `study setup STUDY`
- `STUDY-0001`
- `my notices`

## When you need help

Ask your site's TrialBox administrator; Help on any page gives the address.
