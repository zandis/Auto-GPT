# TrialBox guide for the data steward

Made from the help TrialBox shows on each page, so it says what the screens say. Print it for training; Help shows the latest one.

## What you do

You look after the patients' data: you review break-glass grants, settle conflicts between sources, decide held feed nights, keep the staff and opt-out registers and name each study's principal investigator.

## What TrialBox lets you do

the hold after a restore: see it and release it; accounts: passwords, disable, enable; review every break-glass use; read break-glass grants; keep the opt-out register; keep the staff directory; write a study's PI entry from the signed log; give a CRO account a monitor grant for a visit; pseudonyms in the audit trail; the site's audit trail: filters, one patient's report, chain verification (every read recorded); which records a model, build or prompt touched (every search recorded); the clinical item catalogue; clinical conflicts (read and resolve); data operations: the nights of every source (the steward decides on a held night); record the site's check of criteria not matched

## Your pages

### Home

Your home: the work for your role, most urgent first.

### Feasibility report

How many patients pass each criterion at this hospital, and how many are expected per month. Numbers under the small-cell limit are shown as a range.

### Reports

Finalised feasibility reports of your studies.

### Account requests

Account changes that wait for a second person: confirm or refuse each one. Nobody confirms their own request.

### Break-glass

Emergency access to one patient's record: the grants opened, their reasons, and the review each one needs.

### Audit trail

Who did what, and when: every sign-in, every view of a patient and every change, by person, study or patient.

### Clinical conflicts

Values on which two sources disagree, such as two birth dates. Keep one, with your reason; the trail keeps both.

### Data operations

The hospital's nightly data: each night that was loaded or held, the tables a check named, and your decision on a held night.

### Opt-out register

Patients who said no to research use. Enter a paper or phone opt-out by chart number; the patient leaves every list at once.

### Staff register

The hospital staff who may be named on a study's delegation log, with their department and contact.

### Runs

Every job the box ran or is running, newest first.

### Your account

Your account: studies in scope, language and password.

### Notices received

The notices TrialBox sent you, newest first.

### Notice settings

When and in which language TrialBox mails you.

### Registry

The candidate registry: where the study team is with each pseudonymous patient.

### The command field

What the command field understood, or examples when it did not.

### Ask TrialBox

Ask TrialBox: answers about what to do next and why a patient is on the list, with links.

## When you need help

Ask your site's TrialBox administrator; Help on any page gives the address.
