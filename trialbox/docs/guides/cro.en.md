# TrialBox guide for the CRO specialist (aggregate only)

Made from the help TrialBox shows on each page, so it says what the screens say. Print it for training; Help shows the latest one.

To install the console as an app, choose Install in Edge on Windows, Add to Dock in Safari on macOS, or Share → Add to Home Screen in Safari on iOS. On Android, open the browser menu and choose Install or Add to Home screen. Your device must trust the certificate authority (CA) provided by hospital IT. On an FSLogix virtual desktop, open the console in the browser.

## What you do

You read the sponsor copy of feasibility reports for your studies, nothing else.

## What TrialBox lets you do

aggregate reports only (small counts hidden for a CRO); reports in the sponsor form and the sponsor pack only; read a study's enrolled subjects under a monitor grant (where the study's delegation log allows); record a monitoring visit (date, subjects verified, findings) under a monitor grant (where the study's delegation log allows)

## Your pages

### Home

Your home: the work for your role, most urgent first.

### Feasibility report

How many patients pass each criterion at this hospital, and how many are expected per month. Numbers under the small-cell limit are shown as a range.

### Reports

Finalised feasibility reports of your studies.

### Your account

Your account: studies in scope, language and password.

### The command field

What the command field understood, or examples when it did not.

### Ask TrialBox

Ask TrialBox: answers about what to do next and why a patient is on the list, with links.

## Commands to try

Type one in the command field at the top of any page; STUDY stands for your study's code.

- `reports`
- `STUDY feasibility`
- `my account`
- `help`

## When you need help

Ask your site's TrialBox administrator; Help on any page gives the address.
